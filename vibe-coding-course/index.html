FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about the Vibe Coding with AI live training.

Is this a live in-person course?⌄

Yes — this is a live, in-person training. You'll build real projects during the session with hands-on guidance, not watch pre-recorded videos at home.

Do I need to know how to code?⌄

Zero coding knowledge required. Vibe Coding is specifically designed for non-developers — the whole premise is that you describe what you want in plain language and AI builds it for you.

What experience level is this course for?⌄

The course is for complete beginners to experienced marketers. Whether you've never opened a code editor or you understand the basics, this course will unlock a new level of building speed with AI.

What do I need to bring?⌄

Bring your laptop and a project idea if you have one. Free accounts for Cursor and Claude are helpful to set up beforehand. All other tools, materials, and templates are provided on the day.

Will I actually build something during the course?⌄

Absolutely — that's the whole point. By the end of the training, you'll have a working, deployed project you can show to others. Past participants have shipped landing pages, chatbots, and lead-gen tools on the day.
