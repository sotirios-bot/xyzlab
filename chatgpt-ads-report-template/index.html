What should a ChatGPT Ads report include?

A useful ChatGPT Ads report answers one question: are we hitting our goals, and if not, why not? Every report should include:

  • Spend vs budget — how much was spent against what was available
  • Impressions and click-through rate (CTR) — the reach and relevance of your sponsored placements
  • Cost per result (CPR) — the core performance metric for AI-native ad placements
  • Conversion volume and cost per conversion — the business outcome metric
  • Audience and placement breakdowns — which conversation types and segments drive the best results
  • Week-on-week or month-on-month change — context is everything in performance reporting

Avoid padding reports with raw impression counts unless the client specifically asks. Focus on numbers that connect directly to business outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ChatGPT Ads report template really free?
Yes — completely free. Enter your email to download it. You'll also receive the XYZ Lab newsletter, The Walkthrough, which you can unsubscribe from at any time.
Does it work with Microsoft Excel or just Google Sheets?
Compatible with both Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel. We recommend Google Sheets for the easiest sharing with clients.
How often should I send a ChatGPT Ads report to clients?
Monthly reporting is the standard. For high-spend or test-phase accounts, weekly check-ins using a simplified view of the same data can help catch issues earlier.
Can I use this template for multiple clients?
Yes — make a copy in Google Drive for each client. There's no restriction on how many times you use it.
Do I need to connect any APIs?
No API connection required. The template works with manually exported data. For automated data pulling, you'll need a separate connector tool.
What metrics matter most for ChatGPT Ads?
Focus on CPR (cost per result), CTR and conversion rate — these tell you whether your placements are reaching the right intent and driving the right action.
