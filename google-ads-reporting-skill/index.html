FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about this skill.

What data do I need to share?⌄

Your Google Ads campaign performance data for the reporting period — export directly from Google Ads or paste from a spreadsheet.

Can it compare to previous periods?⌄

Yes. Include data from the comparison period (e.g., last month or same period last year) and the skill will automatically include period-over-period analysis.

Is the report suitable for clients?⌄

Yes. The output is written in plain English suitable for clients, executives, or internal stakeholders with any level of Google Ads knowledge.

Can I customise the report format?⌄

Yes. Tell the skill which sections to include, your brand's reporting style, and any specific KPIs to focus on.
