FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is built in Google Sheets — no extra software, plugins, or paid tools needed. Open it in your browser, paste in your LinkedIn Campaign Manager data, and your report is ready to present to clients or leadership.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use on your next B2B campaign reporting cycle.

Who is this LinkedIn Ads report template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone reporting on LinkedIn advertising performance — B2B marketers, demand generation managers, agency account managers, and in-house marketing teams who need to present Sponsored Content, Lead Gen Form, and audience targeting results clearly to decision-makers without spending hours on formatting.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

12+ pre-built KPIs across 8 fully structured report sections:

  • Campaign performance overview — clicks, impressions, CTR, CPC, CPL, and conversion rate across all active LinkedIn campaign types
  • Sponsored Content breakdown — performance by creative format including single image, carousel, and video with engagement and lead data
  • Lead Gen Form performance — submissions, completion rate, and CPL per form — the core metric for LinkedIn demand generation
  • Audience & targeting analysis — performance segmented by job title, seniority, industry, and company size targeting
  • Month-on-month comparison — automated delta calculations to show growth or flag issues without manual maths
  • Budget pacing tracker — colour-coded pacing status per campaign so you can see at a glance what's on track and what needs attention
  • Insights & recommendations — pre-formatted commentary section for written analysis, next steps, and A/B test findings
  • Executive summary tab — clean one-page stakeholder view with only the headline numbers, ideal for leadership and sales team presentations
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can export data from LinkedIn Campaign Manager and paste it into Google Sheets, you can use this template. No formula-building, no design work — the structure, calculations, and formatting are already built in, including automated MoM comparisons and budget pacing indicators.

How is this different from a generic paid media report template?⌄

It's built specifically around LinkedIn's unique B2B advertising metrics — Lead Gen Form completion rates, audience targeting performance by seniority and job function, Sponsored Content format breakdowns, and CPL benchmarks that matter in a B2B demand generation context. A generic ads template won't capture the nuances of LinkedIn campaign reporting the way this one does.

Can I use this template across multiple clients or campaigns?⌄

Yes. Just duplicate the sheet for each client or account, paste in their Campaign Manager data, and you have a consistent, professional LinkedIn Ads report every time. Agencies managing multiple B2B advertising accounts will find it especially useful for standardising report quality, justifying ad spend to stakeholders, and presenting LinkedIn campaign ROI in a format that decision-makers will actually read — and act on.
