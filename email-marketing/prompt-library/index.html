Common questions about the prompt library.

What AI tools do these prompts work with? ⌄

All prompts are tested with ChatGPT (GPT-4o) and Claude (Sonnet or Opus). They also work with Gemini and other large language models. No special syntax — just copy, fill in the blanks, and paste.

Do I need experience with email marketing to use this? ⌄

No. Every prompt has [PLACEHOLDER] fields you fill in with your own details — your business type, audience, list size, offer, and so on. No copywriting or email marketing experience required. If you can fill in a form, you can use these prompts.

What's included in the full library? ⌄

50 prompts across 8 categories: List Building (6), Campaign Strategy (6), Subject Lines (6), Email Copywriting (7), Automation & Sequences (6), Optimisation & Testing (6), Reporting & Analysis (6), Strategy & Planning (7). Delivered as a PDF and Google Doc so you can copy prompts directly.

Do these prompts work with any email platform? ⌄

Yes. The prompts generate email copy, strategy, and plans — the output is platform-agnostic. Whether you use Mailchimp, Klaviyo, ActiveCampaign, ConvertKit, HubSpot, or any other tool, you can take the AI output and apply it directly.

Generic advice tells you to "write a good subject line." These prompts tell the AI exactly what type of subject line to write, what format the response should be in, and how many options to return — so the first output is ready to use, not a rough draft you have to rewrite.
