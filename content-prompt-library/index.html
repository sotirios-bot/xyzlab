FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the prompt library come in?⌄

The library is delivered as an Excel file (.xlsx), fully compatible with Google Sheets — no extra software needed. Open it, copy any prompt, and paste it straight into ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, or whichever AI tool you use.

How do I receive the library after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access to all 54+ prompts.

Who is the Content Generation AI Prompt Library designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone producing content at any scale — content marketers, social media managers, business owners, freelance creators, and agencies who want to go from blank page to finished content faster, without compromising on quality or brand voice.

What does the prompt library cover?⌄

54+ ready-to-use prompts across 6 core content creation use cases:

  • Content strategy — content pillar definition, topic-to-audience mapping, editorial calendar building, data-rooted strategy frameworks
  • Content ideation — high-potential idea generation from trending topics, competitor gaps, audience questions, and keyword opportunities
  • Image generation — on-brand visual creation with Midjourney and DALL-E for social graphics, ad creatives, blog illustrations, and product shots
  • Video generation — scripts, hooks, storyboards, voiceover briefs for ElevenLabs and Descript, end-to-end video production workflows
  • Social media copy — platform-native captions, hooks, threads, and CTAs for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, and X in your brand voice at scale
  • Content performance analysis — engagement data interpretation, best-performing content pattern identification, audience resonance insights
Do I need paid AI tools or subscriptions to use these prompts?⌄

Most prompts work with free versions of ChatGPT and Claude. Prompts for image and video generation reference tools like Midjourney, DALL-E, ElevenLabs, and Descript — but the library works with whatever AI tools you already have access to. No specific subscription is required to get value from it.

I'm not a professional content creator — will these prompts still work for me?⌄

100%. Whether you're a founder trying to build a brand presence without a creative team, or a freelancer looking to deliver faster for clients, the prompts are ready to use straight out of the file. No copywriting background, design skills, or video production experience needed.

Is this just a list of generic writing prompts repackaged for marketers?⌄

Not at all. These are built specifically for content marketing workflows — think building a 90-day editorial calendar mapped to buyer journey stages, writing platform-native LinkedIn hooks vs. TikTok hooks for the same offer, briefing AI image tools to stay on-brand without a designer, and identifying content decay patterns from engagement data to inform your next content cycle. It's content-specific, built around how modern content creation and distribution actually works.
