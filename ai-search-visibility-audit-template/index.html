What Is an AI Search Visibility Audit?

An AI search visibility audit measures how often, and how accurately, your brand, products or content appear in AI-generated answers. Unlike a standard SEO audit which checks rankings, this audit checks AI platforms directly — running structured prompts and recording what each AI says about your brand.

Why AI Search Visibility Matters Now

AI assistants are increasingly the first place people go for recommendations, comparisons and advice. ChatGPT alone handles over 100 million queries per day, and a growing share of those are commercial queries your brand should appear in. If you're not in the AI answer, you're not in the consideration set.

What should a good AI visibility audit include?

A thorough AI visibility audit goes beyond asking "does AI mention my brand?" It measures accuracy, prominence and consistency across platforms. Here's what to cover:

  • Prompt library — Brand, category and comparison prompts covering your most important use cases
  • Multi-platform testing — Results from ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews
  • Scoring by platform — Visibility score per platform so you can prioritise where to improve first
  • Accuracy check — Whether AI descriptions of your brand are correct, outdated or missing key information
  • Citation tracking — Whether your website is cited as a source when your brand is mentioned
  • Fix checklist — Prioritised actions: crawlability, structured answers, entity clarity and third-party mentions

For guidance on making your content crawlable and citable by AI, see the Google Search Central documentation.

The Prompts to Test Your Brand With

Test three types of prompts. Brand prompts ("What is [brand]?") check how accurately AI describes your business. Category prompts ("Best [category] for [use case]") check whether AI recommends you in relevant searches. Comparison prompts ("[Brand] vs [competitor]") check how AI positions you against alternatives. Run each prompt 2–3 times and note the percentage of runs your brand appears in — single results are unreliable.

How to Score Your AI Visibility

For each prompt: award 2 points if your brand is mentioned first or prominently, 1 point if mentioned but not prominently, and 0 points if not mentioned. Add 1 bonus point if your website is cited as a source. Divide your total by the maximum possible score, multiply by 100, and you have a visibility score out of 100 per platform. The Scoring Sheet in the template does this automatically once you fill in the Audit Tracker.

How to Improve Your AI Search Visibility

Four actions drive the most improvement:

  1. Allow AI crawlers in robots.txt. Add rules permitting ChatGPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. If these bots can't crawl your site, your content can't be cited.
  2. Publish direct-answer content. Write pages that answer specific questions in the first paragraph. AI models quote content that is clear, concise and factually structured.
  3. Build entity consistency. Your brand name, description and key facts should be identical across your website, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, Wikipedia (if applicable) and directory listings. AI models cross-reference multiple sources.
  4. Earn third-party mentions. Citations on authoritative sites, review platforms, industry directories and forums increase the likelihood that AI models include and recommend your brand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI search visibility?
AI search visibility is how often, and how accurately, your brand appears in answers from AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews.
What is the difference between SEO and AEO?
SEO focuses on ranking pages in search results. AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation) focuses on being cited or recommended inside AI-generated answers. AEO builds on SEO foundations such as crawlability, authority and clear content. It also adds entity clarity, structured answers and third-party mentions.
Which AI platforms does the template cover?
ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews and Microsoft Copilot. You can add columns for any other platform your audience uses.
How many prompts should I test?
Start with 20–30 prompts across three groups: brand prompts ("What is [brand]?"), category prompts ("Best [category] for [use case]") and comparison prompts ("[Brand] vs [competitor]"). Re-test the same set monthly to track change.
How do I score my AI visibility?
For each prompt, record whether your brand is mentioned, its position in the answer, whether your site is cited as a source, and whether the description is accurate. The template turns these into a visibility score out of 100 per platform.
Why do AI answers change every time I ask?
AI models generate answers from probability, and some pull live web results. Run each prompt more than once and record the share of runs your brand appears in, rather than relying on a single result.
How often should I run an AI visibility audit?
Monthly for active brands, and after any major content, PR or website change.
How can I improve my AI search visibility?
Make sure AI crawlers can access your site, publish clear answer-style content, add structured data, keep brand information consistent across the web, and earn mentions on trusted third-party sites, review platforms and forums.

Yes. Download it, make a copy, and start your first audit in under an hour.
