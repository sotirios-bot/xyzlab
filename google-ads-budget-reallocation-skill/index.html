FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about this skill.

How much data does the skill need?⌄

At least 30 days of campaign performance data works well. More data (60–90 days) gives more reliable recommendations.

Can I run this for a single campaign?⌄

The skill works best when comparing multiple campaigns, since re-allocation is about moving budget between them. For a single campaign, use the Target CPA Optimisation Skill instead.

Will it recommend pausing campaigns?⌄

Yes — if a campaign is consistently underperforming with no clear path to improvement, the skill will flag it for review or pausing.

How often should I re-run this?⌄

Monthly is ideal for most accounts. Run it after any significant performance change, seasonal shift, or major account restructure.
