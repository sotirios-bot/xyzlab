FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the prompt library come in?⌄

The library is delivered as an Excel file (.xlsx), fully compatible with Google Sheets — no extra software needed. Open it, copy any prompt, and paste it straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or whichever AI tool you use.

How do I receive the library after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access to all 34+ prompts.

Who is the Google Analytics AI Prompt Library designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone working with GA4 data — marketing managers, digital analysts, agency account managers, and business owners who want to stop drowning in dashboards and start making faster, more confident decisions from their website data.

What does the prompt library cover?⌄

34+ ready-to-use prompts across 4 core Google Analytics use cases:

  • Website performance analysis — GA4 traffic reports, session data, engagement metrics, conversion funnel interpretation
  • Actionable insights — top-performing page identification, drop-off point detection, anomaly flagging, data pattern analysis
  • GA4 reporting — plain-English analytics reports from raw exports, stakeholder-ready commentary, trend analysis and recommendations
  • Performance forecasting — projecting future traffic, conversions and revenue trends using historical GA4 data for campaign planning and target-setting
Do I need a paid AI subscription to use these prompts?⌄

No. The prompts work with any AI tool, including free versions of ChatGPT and Claude. No API access, no technical setup — just export your GA4 data, copy a prompt, and go.

I'm not a data analyst — will these prompts still work for me?⌄

Absolutely. That's exactly who these are built for. Whether you're a business owner trying to understand your website traffic or a marketing manager preparing a stakeholder report, the prompts are written to turn raw GA4 exports into clear, plain-English insights — no analytics expertise required.

Is this different from generic data analysis AI prompts?⌄

Yes. These are built specifically for Google Analytics 4 workflows — think interpreting session engagement rates and conversion events, identifying which landing pages are bleeding traffic, generating month-on-month commentary from raw GA4 exports, and forecasting organic traffic trends based on historical data. It's analytics-specific, built around how GA4 actually works.
