FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about this skill.

How much data does the skill need?⌄

At least 30 days of campaign and ad set data. 60–90 days gives more reliable recommendations, especially for accounts with lower conversion volume.

Can it handle Advantage Campaign Budget (ACB)?⌄

Yes. Tell the skill whether you're using ACB or manual ad set budgets and it will adjust its recommendations accordingly.

Will it recommend pausing campaigns?⌄

Yes — if a campaign or ad set is consistently underperforming, the skill will flag it for pausing or restructuring.

How often should I run this?⌄

Monthly at minimum — or after any significant performance change, new creative launch, or audience expansion.
