Setup
Upload Customer Lists CRM Lists to Meta Ads Upload CRM email lists to Meta Ads Audiences and create a Custom Audience to retarget existing contacts on Facebook and Instagram. → Connect Instagram Account to Meta Business Manager Link your Instagram account to Meta Business Manager via Business Settings so you can run ads on both Facebook and Instagram. → Connect WhatsApp Account with Meta Ads Add your WhatsApp Business number to Facebook Business Manager to run click-to-WhatsApp ads. → Connect Meta Ads Official MCP to Claude Link your Meta Ads account to Claude AI so you can analyse and manage campaigns through natural conversation. → Update Payment Method in Meta Ads Open Billing & Payments, go to Payment Methods, select your ad account, and add or update your credit or debit card. → Find Meta Pixel ID Open Events Manager in Meta Business Suite or Business Manager, navigate to Datasets, and copy your Pixel ID. → Find Meta Ads Business Portfolio ID Locate your Meta Business Manager ID (Business Portfolio ID) in Business Settings under Business Info. → Track Events & Custom Conversions with Meta Pixel Use the Event Setup Tool and Custom Conversions to measure the actions that matter most to your business. → Assign Meta Pixel (Dataset) to Meta Ads Account Connect your Meta Pixel (Dataset) to your ad account via Meta Business Settings so conversion tracking works correctly. → Track Meta Ads Events with Event Setup Tool Use the no-code Event Setup Tool in Meta Events Manager to track button clicks and page events without writing any code. → Setup Meta Pixel with Google Tag Manager Meta Pixel Template Install the Meta Pixel via the GTM community template — no manual code, just copy your Pixel ID and publish. → Setup Meta Ads Event Tracking in Go High Level Funnels Install the Meta Pixel and track lead and purchase conversions on your GHL funnel pages. → Track Custom Conversions in Meta Ads Create URL-based custom conversions in Events Manager to measure the exact actions that matter most to your campaigns — no extra code required. →
Optimise
Create Lead Generation Campaigns in Meta Ads Set up a Leads campaign with instant forms to capture contacts directly on Facebook and Instagram — no landing page required. → Add Sitelinks in Meta Ads Add additional clickable links below your Meta ad creative with a destination URL, display label, and 300×300 thumbnail image. → Add Custom Questions to Meta Ads Lead Forms Use the Higher Intent form type to add qualifying custom questions and collect the specific information you need from prospects. → Link Meta Ads Lead Forms with Google Sheets via Zapier Automatically send every Meta Ads lead form submission to a Google Sheets spreadsheet using a Zapier automation. → Bulk Upload Zip Codes (Postal Codes) in Meta Ads Paste a list of postal codes directly into your Ad Set to target multiple locations at once without adding them one by one. → Radius Targeting in Meta Ads Drop a pin anywhere on the map and set a custom radius to target Facebook and Instagram users near any location. → Apply Frequency Cap to Meta Ads Campaigns (Facebook/Instagram Campaigns) Set a frequency cap on your Awareness campaigns to control how often each person sees your Facebook and Instagram ads within a given time window. → Apply UTM Tags in Meta Ads [Full Tutorial] Add UTM parameters directly via URL Parameters in Ads Manager or use the GA4 Campaign URL Builder to track Meta Ads traffic in Google Analytics. → Exclude Audience Network in Meta Ads Remove third-party app and website placements via Placement controls so your ads only run on Facebook and Instagram. → Disable Meta Ads AI Enhancements Turn off Advantage+ creative enhancements at the ad level so your creative is shown exactly as uploaded without AI modifications. → Where to Find Comments on Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram Ads) Access Facebook and Instagram ad comments directly from Ads Manager to moderate and respond. → Difference Between Link Clicks vs Landing Page Views in Meta Ads Learn when to use each metric — Link Clicks for WhatsApp/Messenger, Landing Page Views for website traffic. → Find Change History in Meta Ads View every edit made to your campaigns, ad sets, and ads — including who changed what and when. → Check % of Video Watched in Meta Ads Add Video Plays at 25%, 50%, 75%, 95%, and 100% columns to your Meta Ads report to see how far viewers watch your video ads. →
Report
Download Meta Ads Reports in Excel (CSV) Export your Meta Ads campaign data as a .csv or .xlsx file directly from Ads Manager in just a few clicks. → Schedule Meta Ads Report Delivery Automate report exports and deliver them to your team on a daily, weekly, or monthly schedule. → Find and Download Meta Ads Invoices Access your billing history and download monthly invoices from the Meta Ads Manager Billing & Payments section. → Create Meta Ads Report with Claude [Direct Connection with Meta Ads] Add the Meta Ads MCP custom connector in Claude Settings and generate AI-powered campaign reports through natural conversation. → Meta Ads Benchmarks by Country & Industry (Q2 2026) CTR, CPC, CPM, conversion rate, CPA and ROAS benchmarks across 35 countries and 38 industries — in local currency. →
