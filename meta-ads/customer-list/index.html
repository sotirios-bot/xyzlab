Upload Customer Lists CRM Lists to Meta Ads

Learn how to upload your CRM customer list to Meta Ads as a Custom Audience so you can retarget existing contacts or build lookalike audiences on Facebook and Instagram.

Follow the Steps Book Coaching
Step by Step

Upload Customer Lists CRM Lists to Meta Ads

Follow these steps alongside the video to upload your customer list and create a Custom Audience.

1

Access your Meta Business Manager (Meta Business Suite)

2

Navigate to Audiences (under the "All Tools" menu)

3

Create a Custom Audience

4

Select Customer List

5

Click Next and Select "No" to Customer Value

  • Choose No if you do not have a customer value (lifetime value) column in your list — this is the most common option for a basic email list upload
6

Choose "Paste comma-separated values"

7

Copy-paste your email addresses and click "Next"

  • Paste one email address per line or as a comma-separated list
  • Meta will hash and match your emails to Facebook and Instagram user accounts
8

Proceed and Save your Audience

  • Give your audience a clear, descriptive name so you can identify it easily when setting up Ad Sets
  • It may take up to 30 minutes for the audience to populate and show an estimated size
9

Use the Customer List in your Ad Set

  • When creating an Ad Set, under Custom Audiences select the Customer List Audience you want to use as your ad set's targeting

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about uploading Customer Lists to Meta Ads.

What is a Customer List Custom Audience in Meta Ads? ⌄

A Customer List Custom Audience lets you upload a list of contacts — typically email addresses, phone numbers, or names — from your CRM or email marketing tool. Meta hashes and matches those identifiers against its user database so you can show ads specifically to people already in your customer or prospect list on Facebook and Instagram.

What data can I upload in a Customer List? ⌄

Meta accepts several identifiers including email addresses, phone numbers, first and last names, city, country, date of birth, gender, and mobile advertiser IDs. The more identifiers you include per contact, the higher your match rate will be. Email addresses are the most commonly used and typically yield the best match rates.

How many contacts do I need for a Customer List audience to work? ⌄

Meta requires a minimum matched audience size of 100 people for the audience to be usable in a campaign. In practice, you should aim for at least a few hundred matched contacts for meaningful targeting. Match rates typically range from 40% to 70% depending on the quality and freshness of your data, so upload more contacts than the minimum to ensure you clear the threshold.

Is my customer data secure when I upload it to Meta Ads? ⌄

Meta hashes all personally identifiable information (PII) before it is sent to their servers, meaning your raw contact data is never stored by Meta. The hashing is done locally in your browser before transmission. You should still ensure you have the appropriate legal basis (e.g., consent) to use your contacts' data for advertising purposes in accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable privacy laws.

Can I create a Lookalike Audience from my Customer List? ⌄

Yes — this is one of the most powerful uses of a Customer List. Once your Custom Audience is created and has enough matched users, you can click Create Lookalike Audience from the Audiences dashboard. Meta will find new users on Facebook and Instagram who share similar characteristics to your existing customers, letting you expand your reach to high-quality cold audiences.

What is the "Customer Value" option during upload? ⌄

If you select Yes to Customer Value, Meta will prompt you to include a monetary value column in your upload (e.g., lifetime revenue per customer). This enables you to create a Value-Based Lookalike Audience, where Meta prioritises finding users who resemble your highest-value customers rather than just any customer. Select No if your list is a simple email export without revenue data.
