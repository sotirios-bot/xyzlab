Step by Step

Where to Find Comments on Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram Ads)

Follow these steps alongside the video to access your ad comments.

1

Login to your Meta Ads Manager

2

Navigate to Ads

  • In the left-hand menu, click Ads to view all ads in your account
  • Find the specific ad whose comments you want to view
3

Hover over your ad and click the Edit button

  • Hover your cursor over the ad row to reveal the action icons
  • Click the Edit (pencil) button to open the ad editor
4

Click the arrow button on the top-right of the ad preview

  • In the ad editor, look at the ad preview panel on the right side
  • Click the arrow / external link icon in the top-right corner of the preview to open post options
5

Select "Facebook post with comments" or "Instagram post with comments"

  • A dropdown will appear — choose Facebook post with comments to see your Facebook ad comments
  • Or choose Instagram post with comments to see your Instagram ad comments
  • The post will open in a new tab showing all comments, likes, and engagement on your ad

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about finding and managing comments on Meta Ads.

Can I reply to comments on my Meta Ads directly from Ads Manager?

Not directly from Ads Manager. Once you click Facebook post with comments or Instagram post with comments, you are taken to the live post on Facebook or Instagram where you can reply, like, or hide comments just like any organic post. You can also manage ad comments through Meta Business Suite > Inbox, which consolidates comments from all your posts and ads.

Why should I monitor comments on my Meta Ads?

Ad comments are public and visible to everyone who sees your ad — including potential customers. Negative or unanswered comments can reduce trust and hurt ad performance. Regularly checking comments lets you respond to questions, address complaints quickly, hide spam, and gather feedback on your offer and creative.

Do ad comments disappear when I pause or stop an ad?

No. Comments are tied to the underlying Facebook or Instagram post, not the ad campaign itself. If you pause or stop the ad, the post and its comments remain. However, if you delete the ad and it uses a unique post, the post — and its comments — may also be removed. To preserve comment history, use existing posts as your ad creative rather than single-use ad posts.

Can I hide or delete negative comments on Meta Ads?

Yes. Once you open the post via Facebook post with comments, you can hover over any comment and choose to Hide (visible only to the commenter and their friends) or Delete it entirely. Hiding is generally preferred over deleting for negative comments as it looks less aggressive and the commenter will not know their comment was hidden.

Is there a way to get notified of new comments on my ads automatically?

Yes. In Meta Business Suite, go to Settings > Notifications and enable notifications for comments and messages. You can receive email or mobile push alerts whenever someone comments on one of your ads. You can also set up automated responses in Meta Business Suite to instantly acknowledge new comments or messages while you review them manually.

Do Facebook and Instagram ad comments count separately?

Yes. Facebook and Instagram are separate placements, each with their own comment thread. An ad running on both platforms will have separate comment sections — comments on the Facebook version are not visible on Instagram, and vice versa. Use the Facebook post with comments and Instagram post with comments options separately to view each platform's comments.
