All prompts are tested with ChatGPT (GPT-4o) and Claude (Sonnet or Opus). They also work with Gemini and other large language models. No special syntax — just copy, fill in the blanks, and paste.

No. Every prompt has [PLACEHOLDER] fields you fill in with your own details before pasting. No prompt engineering needed. If you can fill in a form, you can use these prompts.
