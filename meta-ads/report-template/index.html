FAQ

Meta Ads Report Template, FAQ

Common questions about the template.

Does this work in Google Sheets? ⌄

Yes. Upload the Excel file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. All formulas, charts and formatting carry over. You can then share it as a Google Sheets link with clients or teammates.

What's in the AI Prompts tab? ⌄

Six structured prompts: weekly campaign summary, creative fatigue diagnosis, client report narrative, audience and targeting review, budget scaling strategy, and budget allocation recommendation. Each prompt has placeholder fields — add your numbers, paste into ChatGPT or Claude, and receive a written analysis in under 30 seconds.

Do I need Excel skills to use this? ⌄

No. All formulas are already built in. You only enter numbers in the yellow input cells. Everything else — WoW percentages, MoM changes, charts — updates automatically. If you can type a number into a cell, you can use this template.

Can I track multiple ad accounts or campaigns? ⌄

Yes. The template is fully unlocked — no password protection. Duplicate the report tabs for each ad account or campaign you want to track. You can rename each tab to keep things organised across multiple clients or business accounts.

How do I get the template? ⌄

Submit the form and you'll receive the free Excel template by email within a few minutes. No payment required. Check your inbox and spam folder if it doesn't arrive promptly.
