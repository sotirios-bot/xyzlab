Your Questions Answered

What format does the prompt library come in?⌄

The library is delivered as an Excel file (.xlsx), fully compatible with Google Sheets — no extra software needed. Open it, copy any prompt, and paste it straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or whichever AI tool you use.

How do I receive the library after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account creation, no waiting — instant access to all 40+ prompts.

Who is the Meta Ads AI Prompt Library designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone running paid social campaigns — Meta Ads specialists, performance marketers, media buyers, agency account managers, and in-house marketing teams. If you're managing Facebook or Instagram ads and want AI to speed up your workflow, this is for you.

What does the prompt library cover?⌄

40+ ready-to-use prompts across 5 core Meta Ads use cases:

  • Target audience research — ideal customer profiling, interest & behaviour targeting, lookalike strategies, untapped audience segments
  • Account structure — campaign architecture by objective, ad set organisation by audience, testing vs. scaling separation
  • Creatives & ad copy — primary text, headlines, descriptions, hooks, CTAs, UGC-style scripts, and video ad frameworks across multiple angles and formats
  • Reporting — Ads Manager data analysis, plain-English performance summaries, client-ready commentary
  • Optimisation — campaign audits, creative fatigue reviews, budget reallocation priorities, actionable AI-generated recommendations
Do I need a paid AI subscription to use these prompts?⌄

No. The prompts work with any AI tool, including free versions of ChatGPT and Claude. No API access, no paid add-ons — just copy, paste, and go.

I'm new to using AI for paid social — will this work for me?⌄

Absolutely. Every prompt is written specifically for Meta Ads tasks, not repurposed from generic AI templates. Whether you're new to AI or already using it daily, the prompts are ready to use straight out of the file.

Is this different from generic social media AI prompts?⌄

Yes. These are built specifically for Meta Ads workflows — think writing Facebook ad copy across multiple creative angles, building structured Advantage+ campaign setups, analysing cost per result from raw Ads Manager exports, and identifying audience overlap issues.
