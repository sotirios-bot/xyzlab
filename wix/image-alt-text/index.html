Add Alt Text to WIX Product Images

Watch the step-by-step walkthrough below, then follow the guide to add descriptive alt text to every product image in your Wix catalogue to boost SEO and accessibility.

Add Alt Text to WIX Product Images

Follow these steps alongside the video to add alt text to your Wix product images.

1

Login to your WIX backend

2

Navigate to Products, under Catalog from the left-hand side menu

3

Select any product

4

Under Images and Videos, click on the 3 dots (⋮) next to the image

5

Insert the Alt Text and click Done

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about adding alt text to product images in WIX.

What is alt text and why does it matter for SEO? ⌄

Alt text (alternative text) is a short description of an image that search engines read to understand what the image shows. Since Google cannot "see" images, it relies on alt text to index them correctly. Well-written alt text helps your product images appear in Google Image Search, contributes to your page's keyword relevance, and improves accessibility for visually impaired visitors using screen readers.

How should I write good alt text for product images? ⌄

Good alt text is descriptive and specific. Include the product name, key attributes (colour, material, size), and any relevant context. For example: "Blue leather crossbody bag with gold zip" is far better than "bag" or "IMG_1234". Keep it under 125 characters and avoid keyword stuffing — write for a human who can't see the image.

Does alt text affect my Wix store's Google ranking? ⌄

Yes, indirectly. Alt text helps Google understand the context of your product pages, which can improve their relevance for related search queries. Product images with keyword-rich alt text also have a better chance of appearing in Google Shopping image panels and image search results — both of which drive additional organic traffic to your store.

Do I need to add alt text to every product image? ⌄

Ideally yes, but prioritise your primary product image first as it carries the most weight. Additional gallery images (angle shots, lifestyle photos) should also have alt text, but if you have a large catalogue, start with the main image for each product and work through the rest over time.

Can I add alt text to regular page images (not just product images) in Wix? ⌄

Yes. For images placed directly on Wix pages (not in the product catalogue), click the image in the Wix Editor, select Settings, and you'll find an Alt Text field. This process is separate from adding alt text to product catalogue images, which is done via the backend as shown in this tutorial.

Does Wix automatically generate alt text for product images? ⌄

Wix may use the product name as a fallback alt text if none is provided, but it is not guaranteed and the auto-generated text is often generic. For best SEO results, always write your own alt text manually — it takes just a few seconds per image and makes a meaningful difference to how Google indexes your products.
