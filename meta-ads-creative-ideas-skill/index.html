FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about this skill.

Does it produce the actual creatives?⌄

No — it produces creative briefs and concept outlines. You then brief a designer, use a tool like Canva, or produce the content yourself.

How many concepts does it generate?⌄

Typically 5–8 creative concepts per run, each with multiple hook and headline variations.

Can I request a specific type of creative?⌄

Yes. Tell the skill what format you need (video, static, UGC-style) and it will tailor the concepts accordingly.

What if my account doesn't have much data?⌄

The skill can work with minimal data — just include your product details, competitor context, and any audience feedback you have.
