What's Covered

Everything You Need to Set Up and Audit GA4 Correctly

From initial property setup to regular audit items — this checklist ensures your GA4 data is accurate, complete, and actionable.

✓
Account & Property Setup

Property configuration, account structure, user access levels, and data retention settings — the foundation of a trustworthy GA4 implementation.

✓
Data Stream Configuration

Web and app stream setup, Google tag verification, cross-domain tracking, and enhanced measurement settings confirmed and working.

✓
Goals & Conversion Events

Key conversion events marked, thank-you page triggers verified, and conversion values assigned so every goal is measurable.

✓
Custom Event Tracking

Scroll depth, button clicks, form submissions, video plays, and file downloads tracked via GTM or direct implementation.

✓
Audience & Segment Building

Key audiences created — new vs. returning, converters, high-value users — and synced to Google Ads for remarketing.

✓
Filters, Exclusions & Data Quality

Internal traffic filtered, bot traffic excluded, hostname filters applied — so your data only reflects real users on your site.

✓
Looker Studio Connection

GA4 connected to Looker Studio with the right date ranges, dimensions, and metrics configured for your reporting needs.

✓
Regular Audit Items

Monthly checks to catch data discrepancies, channel attribution changes, and new tracking gaps before they affect your decisions.
