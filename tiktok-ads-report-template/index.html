FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is built for TikTok Ads Manager data and delivered as a downloadable file compatible with Google Sheets — no extra software, plugins, or paid tools needed. Open it, paste in your campaign export, and your report is ready to present.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use on your next reporting cycle.

Who is this TikTok Ads report template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone reporting on TikTok advertising performance — TikTok Ads specialists, social media agency account managers, and DTC brands who want to present video ad results, conversion data, and creative performance clearly without cobbling together screenshots from Ads Manager every month.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

10+ pre-built metrics across 8 fully structured report sections:

  • Campaign overview — total spend, reach, impressions, and results at campaign level with automatic MoM change calculations
  • Ad group performance — audience segment and placement-level breakdown to identify best cost per result
  • Creative metrics — view rate, CTR, and 6-second view rate per creative to surface scroll-stopping hooks
  • CPM / CPC / CPR tracker — key cost metrics over time with automatic month-on-month comparisons built in
  • Video completion rate analysis — 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100% completion rates tracked by creative to measure content quality
  • Audience insights snapshot — age, gender, and device breakdowns showing who your TikTok ads are actually reaching and converting
  • Spend vs. results summary — total spend mapped against conversions, CPR trend, and budget pacing in one overview
  • Optimisation recommendations — pre-formatted commentary section for creative testing conclusions and next-month action plan
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can export data from TikTok Ads Manager and paste it into a spreadsheet, you can use this template. No formula-building, no design work — the structure, calculations, and formatting are already done, including video-specific metrics you won't find in generic ads report templates.

How is this different from a generic paid social report template?⌄

It's built specifically around TikTok's unique performance metrics — video completion rates, hook performance, 6-second view rates, and scroll-stopping CTR data that don't exist in Meta or Google Ads reporting. If you're running TikTok campaigns, you need a template that reflects how TikTok actually measures success, not one borrowed from another platform.

Can I use this template across multiple client accounts?⌄

Yes. Just duplicate the sheet for each client, paste in their TikTok Ads Manager export, and you have a consistent, professional report every time. Agencies managing multiple TikTok advertising accounts will find it especially useful for standardising reporting quality, cutting turnaround time, and delivering results in a format clients can actually understand — without the chaos of raw Ads Manager data.
