The Problem

Most LinkedIn budgets die
in the first two weeks.

✕
Wrong campaign objective — Brand Awareness and Website Visits look good in reports. They rarely reach your bank account.
✕
Audience Expansion left on — LinkedIn takes your targeting as a suggestion and shows your ads to people you never intended to reach.
✕
No conversion tracking — You know your spend. You don't know your cost per qualified lead. Impossible to improve what you can't measure.

This playbook closes every leak — with the exact settings to use and avoid.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is this for?

Anyone running or planning to run LinkedIn Ads — B2B companies, consultants, agencies and founders who want to reach decision-makers without guessing. Beginner-friendly enough to follow from day one, and detailed enough to be useful for experienced marketers who want a second opinion on their setup.

How much should I be spending to use this?

The playbook recommends starting at $30/day — that's the practical minimum for LinkedIn's algorithm to learn who converts. At less than that, you get one or two clicks a day and no useful data. You don't need a big budget to start, but you need enough of it — and the patience to let it work for at least two weeks.

I'm already running LinkedIn Ads. Will I still learn something?

Almost certainly. The most common issues in existing LinkedIn accounts are Audience Expansion being left on, the wrong objective selected, and no clear cost-per-qualified-lead target. The playbook covers all three, plus the weekly optimisation routine and 10 rules most experienced advertisers haven't systematised.

Is it up to date?

Yes — this is the 2026 Edition, updated to reflect current Campaign Manager settings, objectives, ad formats and LinkedIn's latest recommendations. The benchmarks cover CPC, CPM and cost per lead across 11 world regions with 2026 data.

Does it include creative guidance?

Yes — Section 06 covers every ad format (what works, what to avoid), creative specs, the Thought Leader Ads strategy, single image best practices with a worked example, copy rules and 5 fill-in-the-blank hook formulas. Section 07 covers Lead Gen Forms and the 5–7 email follow-up sequence.
