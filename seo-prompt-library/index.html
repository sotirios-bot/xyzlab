FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the prompt library come in?⌄

The library is delivered as an Excel file (.xlsx), fully compatible with Google Sheets — no extra software needed. Open it, copy any prompt, and paste it straight into ChatGPT, Claude, or whichever AI tool you use.

How do I receive the library after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access to all 42+ prompts.

Who is the SEO AI Prompt Library designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone working on organic search — SEO specialists, consultants, agency account managers, content marketers, and copywriters who want to move faster on audits, optimisation, and reporting without sacrificing quality or strategic thinking.

What does the prompt library cover?⌄

42+ ready-to-use prompts across 5 core SEO use cases:

  • On-page SEO auditing — title tag analysis, meta description review, heading structure, internal linking gaps, content gap identification
  • On-page SEO implementation — rewriting title tags, generating meta descriptions at scale, restructuring headings, improving anchor text, producing schema markup
  • Search-friendly content creation — keyword-mapped content briefs, SEO-optimised blog posts, FAQ sections, search intent matching
  • SEO reporting — Search Console and GA4 export analysis, plain-English ranking summaries, trend commentary, client-ready recommendations
  • SEO performance analysis — organic traffic trend interpretation, ranking opportunity identification, declining visibility detection, prioritised action plans
Do I need a paid AI subscription to use these prompts?⌄

No. The prompts work with any AI tool, including free versions of ChatGPT and Claude. No technical setup, no API access — just bring your data or URL, copy a prompt, and go.

I'm a content writer, not a technical SEO — will these prompts work for me?⌄

Absolutely. The library covers the full spectrum — from technical on-page fixes to content briefs and blog writing. Whether you're rewriting meta descriptions or producing long-form content that ranks, there are prompts built specifically for your workflow, no SEO certification required.

Is this just generic content writing advice repackaged as SEO prompts?⌄

Not at all. These are built specifically for organic search workflows — think analysing crawl data to find indexation issues, generating schema markup for structured data, interpreting keyword ranking movements from Search Console exports, identifying pages losing visibility due to algorithm updates, and writing content briefs mapped to specific search intent. It's SEO-specific, built around how modern search engine optimisation actually works.
