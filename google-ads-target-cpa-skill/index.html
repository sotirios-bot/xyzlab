FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about this skill.

How much data does the skill need?⌄

Ideally 30–90 days of conversion data. Campaigns with fewer than 30 conversions in the period will receive a more conservative estimate.

What if I'm not using Target CPA bidding?⌄

The skill will also recommend whether to switch to Target CPA, Target ROAS, or Maximise Conversions based on your data and goals.

How often should I update my CPA targets?⌄

Review and adjust targets monthly — or whenever conversion volume, seasonality, or business economics change significantly.

Can it handle multiple campaigns at once?⌄

Yes. Paste data from your entire account and the skill will provide targets for every campaign in a single output.
