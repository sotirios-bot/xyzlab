Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before joining an XYZ Lab course.

Are these live in-person classes or online?⌄

All XYZ Lab courses are delivered as live, in-person training sessions in Singapore. You'll learn in a focused small-group environment with direct access to your trainer — no recordings, no self-paced modules.

Where are the training sessions held?⌄

Training is held at our Singapore office: 160 Robinson Road, #14-04 Business Federation Centre, Singapore 068914 — centrally located in the CBD, a short walk from Tanjong Pagar MRT.

Do I need prior experience with AI tools?⌄

No prior AI experience is required. Our courses are designed for marketers and business owners at all levels. We cover the fundamentals and get you using AI tools confidently from day one.

How long does each course run?⌄

Most courses run over 1–2 full training days depending on subject depth. Each session includes hands-on exercises and real-world projects. Check individual course pages for exact session details.

Will I receive a certificate?⌄

Yes — every participant receives an XYZ Lab certificate of completion after finishing a course, perfect for your LinkedIn profile and professional portfolio.

Can my whole team attend together?⌄

Absolutely. We offer group rates for teams of 3 or more and can arrange private in-house sessions tailored to your company's tools and goals. Get in touch to discuss.

What is your cancellation and refund policy?⌄

We offer a full refund if you cancel at least 7 days before the course date. Cancellations within 7 days can be transferred to a future session. Email hello@xyzlab.com with any queries.
