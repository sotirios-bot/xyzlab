FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can we go through multiple channels in one session?

Yes — we can cover as many channels as your session allows. Most clients come in with a mix of Google Ads and Meta Ads, and we work through whichever platforms are most relevant to your business. If you're running campaigns across TikTok, LinkedIn, or other channels, we can include those too. We'll prioritise based on where the biggest opportunities or pain points are, and make sure every minute of the session drives value for your specific setup.

Will the coaching session be recorded?

Yes — the full session is recorded and shared with you afterwards. This means you can focus entirely on the conversation during the call, ask questions freely, and revisit any part of the review at your own pace. Many clients find it useful to rewatch specific sections when implementing the recommendations, especially the more technical account changes.

Will the session notes be shared?

Yes — after the session you'll receive a written summary covering the key findings, prioritised action items, and the specific changes we discussed. The notes are structured so you can hand them off to a team member or use them as a checklist when implementing improvements. Nothing gets lost or forgotten — everything we cover is documented for you.

What if I have questions after the session?

You can always reach out after the session and we'll do our best to answer your questions. Whether something comes up during implementation or you want a second opinion on a decision, don't hesitate to get in touch. We want the session to keep delivering value long after the call ends — not just in the moment.
