FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is delivered as a downloadable file compatible with Google Sheets — no extra software, plugins, or paid tools needed. Open it in your browser, paste in your Ads Manager data, and your report is ready to present across both Facebook and Instagram campaigns.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use before your next client reporting cycle.

Who is this Meta Ads report template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone reporting on paid social performance — Meta Ads specialists, social media agency account managers, and ecommerce brands who want to present Facebook and Instagram campaign results clearly and professionally without rebuilding the format from scratch every month.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

10+ pre-built KPIs across 8 fully structured report sections:

  • Campaign performance overview — spend, reach, impressions, clicks, CTR, and ROAS across all active campaigns in one view
  • Ad set breakdown — audience-level performance to identify which targeting segments are delivering and which are burning budget
  • Creative performance analysis — side-by-side ad creative comparison with CTR, hook rate, and cost per result to surface winning formats
  • Audience reach & frequency — unique reach and frequency tracking with automatic fatigue alerts
  • CPM / CPC / CPR tracker — key cost metrics over time with automatic month-on-month comparison for efficiency trend spotting
  • ROAS by campaign — return on ad spend broken down by prospecting, retargeting, and retention campaigns
  • A/B test results summary — dedicated section to log split test findings, winning variants, and next actions
  • Recommendations & next steps — pre-formatted commentary block for written analysis and a clear action plan
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can export data from Meta Ads Manager and paste it into a spreadsheet, you can use this template. No formula-building, no design work — the structure, calculations, and formatting are already built in for both Facebook and Instagram campaign data.

How much time will this actually save me?⌄

The template is built to save around 2 hours per report. Instead of manually pulling metrics from Ads Manager, calculating MoM changes, and formatting everything from scratch, you paste in your data and the report structures itself — leaving you to focus on the creative insights and strategic recommendations, not the spreadsheet.

Can I use this template across multiple client accounts?⌄

Yes. Just duplicate the sheet for each client, paste in their Ads Manager export, and you have a consistent, professional Meta Ads report every time. Agencies managing multiple paid social accounts will find it especially useful for standardising reporting quality, reducing turnaround time, and presenting results in a format clients will actually read — and trust.
