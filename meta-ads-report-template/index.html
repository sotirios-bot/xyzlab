What Is a Meta Ads Reporting Dashboard?

A Meta Ads reporting dashboard is a summary view of your Facebook and Instagram ad account. It aggregates raw campaign data into key metrics — ROAS, CPA, CPM, CTR and conversions — so you can communicate performance clearly without manually building charts each month.

The dashboard in this template is designed for monthly reporting. You export data from Meta Ads Manager, paste it into the Raw Data tab, and the dashboard updates automatically.

Which Meta Ads Metrics Should You Report?

The metrics that matter depend on your campaign objective. For conversion campaigns, the most important metrics are ROAS, CPA (cost per result) and conversion volume. For awareness campaigns, track CPM, reach and frequency. For traffic campaigns, track link CTR, CPC and landing page views.

The template includes all of these, with conditional formatting that flags campaigns performing below target on ROAS and CPA — so the problem areas are visible at a glance before you go into campaign-level detail.

What should a good Meta Ads dashboard include?

A well-structured Meta Ads dashboard makes it easy to answer: how is the account performing, where is the spend going, and what needs to change? Here's what to include:

  • KPI tiles — Total spend, ROAS, CPA and conversions with period-over-period change
  • Campaign performance table — One row per campaign showing spend, CPM, CTR, CPC, conversions and ROAS
  • Ad-set breakdown — Where budget is concentrated and which audiences are performing
  • Trend chart — Spend and ROAS plotted over time to show whether performance is improving
  • Placement breakdown — How Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Stories and Reels compare
  • Target vs. actual flags — Clear indicators showing whether campaigns hit your ROAS or CPA goal

How to Benchmark Your Meta Ads Performance

Benchmarks give you context for your numbers. Average Meta Ads benchmarks across industries sit around 0.9–1.2% link CTR, £8–15 CPM and £1.50–4.00 CPC, but these vary significantly by industry, objective and audience. The most useful benchmark is your own account's historical performance — if ROAS was 4× last month and it's 2× this month with the same budget, that's the signal worth investigating, not whether your CPM is above an industry average.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Meta Ads reporting dashboard?
A Meta Ads reporting dashboard is a visual summary of your Facebook and Instagram ad performance. It shows key metrics like ROAS, CPA, CPM, CTR and spend in one place, so you can spot trends and report results to clients or stakeholders quickly.
What metrics does the template track?
The template tracks spend, impressions, reach, CPM, link clicks, CTR (all and link), CPC, landing page views, results (conversions), CPA (cost per result), ROAS and 7-day click conversions — the most common metrics for Meta Ads reporting.
Does the template work for Facebook and Instagram?
Yes. Meta Ads Manager combines Facebook and Instagram data in the same export, so the template covers both. You can filter by placement inside the dashboard using the placement breakdown column.
Can I use this for client reporting?
Yes. The template is designed for agency and freelancer use. Add your client's logo to the cover tab, fill in the account details, and share view-only access via Google Sheets. You can also export a PDF for email delivery.
How do I export data from Meta Ads Manager?
In Meta Ads Manager, click Ads Reporting, then Export Table Data and select CSV. Choose the columns you want to include. The template's Raw Data tab is formatted to match a standard Meta export.
What is a good ROAS for Meta Ads?
A commonly cited benchmark is 3–4× ROAS (£3–4 revenue for every £1 spent), but the right target depends on your margin. The template includes a target ROAS input so you can flag campaigns that fall below your threshold.
Can I track multiple campaigns in one dashboard?
Yes. The campaign performance table shows each campaign as a separate row, so you can compare ROAS, CPA and CTR across all active campaigns in one view.
Does the template include a MoM trend chart?
Yes. The Monthly Trends tab plots spend, conversions and ROAS month-over-month so you can present performance trends visually in client reviews.
