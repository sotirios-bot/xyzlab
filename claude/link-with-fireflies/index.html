FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about linking Fireflies with Claude.

What is Fireflies.ai and what does linking it with Claude do?

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting assistant that automatically records, transcribes, and summarises your meetings across video conferencing platforms. Linking it with Claude lets you search transcripts, retrieve meeting summaries, and extract action items directly inside Claude conversations — so you can use your meeting data to draft emails, reports, and follow-ups without switching tabs.

Where do I find the Connectors option in Claude?

Open Claude and click the Customize button (usually found in the sidebar or navigation menu). Inside the Customize panel you will see a Connectors section listing all available third-party integrations, including Fireflies.

Do I need a paid Fireflies plan to connect it with Claude?

You need an active Fireflies.ai account to authorise the connection. API access for integrations may require a paid Fireflies plan — check Fireflies.ai's pricing page for the latest details on which tiers include connector and API access.

What can I do inside Claude after linking Fireflies?

Once connected, you can ask Claude to retrieve transcripts from recent meetings, summarise key discussion points, pull out action items and decisions, and use that meeting context when drafting follow-up emails, project updates, or client reports — all without leaving Claude.

Is my Fireflies meeting data shared with Claude automatically?

No. Claude only accesses your Fireflies data when you explicitly ask it to in a conversation. The connector grants permission for Claude to query Fireflies on your behalf, but transcripts and summaries are fetched on-demand rather than synced continuously.

Can I disconnect Fireflies from Claude later?

Yes. Return to Customize > Connectors in Claude, find Fireflies in your connected integrations, and select the option to disconnect or revoke access. You can also revoke the connection from within your Fireflies.ai account settings under connected apps or integrations.
