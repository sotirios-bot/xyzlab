Link WIX to Claude

Follow these steps alongside the video to connect WIX to your Claude account.

1

Navigate to Settings

2

Select Connectors

3

Find WIX, select Configure, login and authorise

  • Scroll through the connectors list or search for WIX
  • Click Configure next to WIX
  • Login to your WIX account when prompted
  • Click Authorise to grant Claude access to your WIX website

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about connecting WIX to Claude.

What can I do after linking WIX to Claude? ⌄

Once connected, Claude can interact with your WIX website directly from the conversation window. You can ask Claude to read site content, manage blog posts, update product listings, access contact information, and query your site data — all without leaving Claude. This lets you use AI to streamline content management, update your site faster, and get answers about your WIX data in natural language.

Where is the Connectors menu in Claude? ⌄

Go to Settings in your Claude account (accessible from the sidebar or profile menu at claude.ai), then look for the Connectors section. This is where all third-party integrations are managed, including WIX, Asana, GitHub, and others.

Which WIX plan is required to connect to Claude? ⌄

The WIX integration in Claude works via WIX's API, which is available on paid WIX plans. Free WIX accounts may have limited API access. If you run into issues connecting, check that your WIX site is on a plan that supports third-party API integrations. Refer to WIX's documentation for the most up-to-date plan requirements.

Can Claude update my WIX website content on my behalf? ⌄

Yes. Once authorised, Claude can interact with your WIX site through the integration. The exact capabilities depend on the permissions granted during the Configure step. You can ask Claude to retrieve content, manage collections, and query your WIX CMS data in natural language — making it easy to update your site without navigating the WIX dashboard.

How do I disconnect WIX from Claude? ⌄

Go back to Settings → Connectors in Claude and find WIX in your connected integrations. Click the disconnect or remove option to revoke access. You can also manage third-party app permissions directly from your WIX dashboard under Settings → Integrations.

Is the WIX connector available on the free Claude plan? ⌄

Connector availability in Claude depends on your plan and region. If WIX appears in your Settings → Connectors list, it is available for your account. Claude Pro subscribers generally have access to more connectors and higher usage limits. Check the Connectors section in your account to see what is currently available.
