Link Miro with Claude

Follow these steps alongside the video to connect Miro to your Claude account.

1

Navigate to Settings

2

Select Connectors

3

Find Miro, select Connect, login and authorise

  • Scroll through the connectors list or search for Miro
  • Click Connect next to Miro
  • Login to your Miro account when prompted
  • Click Authorise to grant Claude access to your Miro boards and workspaces

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about connecting Miro to Claude.

What can I do after linking Miro with Claude? ⌄

Once connected, Claude can interact with your Miro workspace directly from the conversation window. You can ask Claude to create new boards, add sticky notes and cards, read existing board content, and manage your visual workflows — all without leaving Claude. This lets you use AI to speed up brainstorming, build diagrams, and organise ideas on Miro boards in natural language.

Where is the Connectors menu in Claude? ⌄

Go to Settings in your Claude account (accessible from the sidebar or profile menu at claude.ai), then look for the Connectors section. This is where all third-party integrations are managed, including Miro, Asana, Apollo.io, and others.

Which Miro plan is required to connect to Claude? ⌄

The Miro integration in Claude works via Miro's API. Free Miro accounts have limited board and API access. For full functionality — including creating boards and accessing team workspaces — a paid Miro plan is recommended. Refer to Miro's documentation for the most up-to-date API and plan requirements.

Can Claude create and edit Miro boards for me? ⌄

Yes. Once authorised, Claude can interact with your Miro workspace through the integration. You can ask Claude to create new boards, add content items like sticky notes or text, read board data, and retrieve workspace information in natural language — making it faster to set up and populate boards without opening Miro directly.

How do I disconnect Miro from Claude? ⌄

Go back to Settings → Connectors in Claude and find Miro in your connected integrations. Click the disconnect or remove option to revoke access. You can also manage third-party app permissions directly from your Miro account under Profile → Apps.

Is the Miro connector available on the free Claude plan? ⌄

Connector availability in Claude depends on your plan and region. If Miro appears in your Settings → Connectors list, it is available for your account. Claude Pro subscribers generally have access to more connectors and higher usage limits. Check the Connectors section in your account to see what is currently available.
