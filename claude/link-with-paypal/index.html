FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about linking PayPal to Claude.

What does linking PayPal to Claude do?

Linking PayPal to Claude lets you query your PayPal account data directly inside Claude conversations — looking up transactions, checking payment history, retrieving invoice details, and using that financial context when drafting reports, reconciliation summaries, or client invoices without switching between tools.

Where do I find the Connectors option in Claude?

Open Claude and click the Customize button (usually found in the sidebar or navigation menu). Inside the Customize panel you will see a Connectors section listing all available third-party integrations, including PayPal.

Do I need a business PayPal account to connect it to Claude?

You need an active PayPal account to authorise the connection. A PayPal Business account may be required for full API access — check PayPal's developer documentation for the latest details on which account types support third-party integrations.

What can I do inside Claude after linking PayPal?

Once connected, you can ask Claude to look up recent transactions, retrieve payment details for specific customers, check subscription statuses, and use that payment data when drafting revenue reports, refund summaries, or financial overviews — all without leaving Claude.

Is my PayPal transaction data shared with Claude automatically?

No. Claude only accesses your PayPal data when you explicitly ask it to in a conversation. The connector grants permission for Claude to query PayPal on your behalf, but transaction data is fetched on-demand rather than synced continuously.

Can I disconnect PayPal from Claude later?

Yes. Return to Customize > Connectors in Claude, find PayPal in your connected integrations, and select the option to disconnect or revoke access. You can also revoke the connection from within your PayPal account settings under connected apps or third-party access.
