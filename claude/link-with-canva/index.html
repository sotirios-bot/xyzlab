FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about connecting Canva to Claude.

What can I do after linking Canva to Claude? ⌄

Once connected, Claude can interact with your Canva account directly from the conversation window. You can ask Claude to create new designs, open existing ones, generate design briefs, and get AI-powered suggestions — all without switching tabs. The integration allows Claude to act as a design co-pilot, handling visual creation tasks alongside text-based work.

Do I need a paid Canva account to connect it to Claude? ⌄

No, a free Canva account is sufficient to connect and use the integration with Claude. However, certain Canva features used within the workflow (such as premium templates or Brand Kit elements) will still require a Canva Pro subscription to access.

Where is the Customize menu in Claude? ⌄

The Customize option appears in the left-hand sidebar when you are logged into Claude at claude.ai. It sits below your conversation history. Clicking it opens a panel where you can manage integrations, set custom instructions, and configure connected tools including Canva, GitHub, and others.

Can I disconnect Canva from Claude at any time? ⌄

Yes. To disconnect, go back to Customize → Connect your tools, find Canva in the list of connected tools, and click the disconnect or revoke access option. You can also revoke Claude's access from within your Canva account settings under Connected Apps.

Is the Canva integration available on the free Claude plan? ⌄

Integrations and the ability to connect external tools in Claude are available depending on your plan and region. Check the Connect your tools section in your Claude account — if Canva appears as an option, it is available for your plan. Claude Pro subscribers generally have access to a wider range of integrations and higher usage limits.

What other tools can I connect to Claude? ⌄

Claude supports a growing list of integrations via the Connect your tools panel, including GitHub, Google Drive, Notion, Jira, and more. Each integration allows Claude to read or interact with data from the connected platform — for example, referencing your code repository or reading documents from Google Drive directly within a conversation.
