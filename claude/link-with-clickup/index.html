FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about connecting ClickUp to Claude.

What can I do after linking ClickUp to Claude? ⌄

Once connected, Claude can read and interact with your ClickUp workspace directly from the conversation window. You can ask Claude to create tasks, update statuses, list overdue items, summarise project progress, and generate reports — without leaving Claude. This lets you use AI to plan, prioritise, and organise your work in ClickUp alongside everything else you do in Claude.

Where is the Connectors menu in Claude? ⌄

Go to Settings in your Claude account (accessible from the sidebar or profile menu at claude.ai), then look for the Connectors section. This is where all third-party integrations are managed, including ClickUp, Asana, GitHub, Canva, and others.

Does the ClickUp integration work with free ClickUp accounts? ⌄

Yes, you can connect a free ClickUp account to Claude. The integration works through ClickUp's standard OAuth authentication. However, the tasks and workspaces you can access via Claude will be limited to what your ClickUp plan allows — features such as advanced automations, goals, and dashboards are only available on paid ClickUp tiers.

Can Claude create and assign ClickUp tasks on my behalf? ⌄

Yes. Once authorised, Claude can create new tasks, set due dates, assign them to team members, and add them to lists or folders in ClickUp. You can simply describe what you need in natural language — for example, "Create a task called 'Review campaign brief' in the Marketing space, due this Friday, assigned to Sarah" — and Claude will handle it.

How do I disconnect ClickUp from Claude? ⌄

Go back to Settings → Connectors in Claude and find ClickUp in your connected integrations. Click the disconnect or revoke option to remove access. You can also revoke Claude's access directly from your ClickUp account under Settings → Integrations, where all connected third-party apps are listed.

Is the ClickUp connector available on the free Claude plan? ⌄

Connector availability in Claude depends on your plan and region. If ClickUp appears in your Settings → Connectors list, it is available for your account. Claude Pro subscribers generally have access to more connectors and higher usage limits for integrated tools. Check the Connectors section in your account to see what is currently available.
