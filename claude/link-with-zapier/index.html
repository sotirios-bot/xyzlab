Link Zapier to Claude

Follow these steps alongside the video to connect Zapier to your Claude account.

1

Navigate to Settings

2

Select Connectors

3

Find Zapier, select Connect, login and authorise

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about connecting Zapier to Claude.

What can I do after linking Zapier to Claude? ⌄

Once connected, Claude can interact with your Zapier account directly from the conversation window. You can ask Claude to trigger Zaps, list your automations, run workflows, and manage connected apps — all without leaving Claude. This lets you use AI to kick off automations on demand, test workflows, and orchestrate tasks across hundreds of apps that Zapier connects to.

Where is the Connectors menu in Claude? ⌄

Go to Settings in your Claude account (accessible from the sidebar or profile menu at claude.ai), then look for the Connectors section. This is where all third-party integrations are managed, including Zapier, Ahrefs, Miro, and others.

Which Zapier plan is required to connect to Claude? ⌄

The Zapier integration in Claude works via Zapier's API. While Zapier has a free tier, triggering Zaps programmatically and using multi-step automations may require a paid Zapier plan. Refer to Zapier's documentation for the most up-to-date plan and API requirements.

Can Claude trigger my existing Zaps from a conversation? ⌄

Yes. Once authorised, Claude can interact with your Zapier workflows through the integration. You can ask Claude to trigger specific Zaps, list available automations, and run workflows on demand in natural language — making it easy to kick off multi-step automations across all your connected apps without switching tools.

How do I disconnect Zapier from Claude? ⌄

Go back to Settings → Connectors in Claude and find Zapier in your connected integrations. Click the disconnect or remove option to revoke access. You can also manage connected app permissions from your Zapier account under Settings → Connected Accounts.

Is the Zapier connector available on the free Claude plan? ⌄

Connector availability in Claude depends on your plan and region. If Zapier appears in your Settings → Connectors list, it is available for your account. Claude Pro subscribers generally have access to more connectors and higher usage limits. Check the Connectors section in your account to see what is currently available.
