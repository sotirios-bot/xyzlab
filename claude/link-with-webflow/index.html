FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about linking Webflow to Claude.

What is Webflow and what does linking it to Claude do? ⌄

Webflow is a visual web design platform that lets you build, manage, and publish websites without writing code. Linking it to Claude lets you interact with your Webflow site and CMS data directly inside Claude conversations — querying content, updating CMS items, and managing collections without leaving the chat interface.

Where do I find the Connectors option in Claude? ⌄

Open Claude and click the Customize button (usually found in the sidebar or navigation menu). Inside the Customize panel you will see a Connectors section that lists all available third-party integrations, including Webflow.

Do I need a paid Webflow plan to connect it to Claude? ⌄

You need an active Webflow account to authorise the connection. Access to certain features like the CMS API may depend on your Webflow plan — check Webflow's pricing page for the latest details on API access per plan tier.

What can I do inside Claude after linking Webflow? ⌄

Once connected, you can ask Claude to retrieve CMS collection items, create or update content entries, query site metadata, and use your Webflow data as context when drafting copy, blog posts, or product descriptions — all without switching between tools.

Is my Webflow site data shared with Claude automatically? ⌄

No. Claude only accesses your Webflow data when you explicitly ask it to in a conversation. The connector grants permission for Claude to query Webflow on your behalf, but data is fetched on-demand rather than synced continuously.

Can I disconnect Webflow from Claude later? ⌄

Yes. Return to Customize > Connectors in Claude, find Webflow in your connected integrations, and select the option to disconnect or revoke access. You can also revoke the connection from within your Webflow account settings under authorised applications.
