FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about linking Granola to Claude.

What is Granola and what does linking it to Claude do?

Granola is an AI-powered meeting notes app that automatically transcribes and summarises your meetings. Linking it to Claude lets you access your Granola meeting notes and summaries directly inside Claude conversations, so you can ask questions, extract action items, or use meeting context when drafting emails and documents.

Where do I find the Connectors option in Claude?

Open Claude and click the Customize button (usually found in the sidebar or navigation menu). Inside the Customize panel you will see a Connectors section that lists all available third-party integrations, including Granola.

Do I need a paid Granola plan to connect it to Claude?

You need an active Granola account to authorise the connection. Whether a specific paid tier is required depends on Granola's current plan structure — check Granola's pricing page for the latest connector access details.

What can I do inside Claude after linking Granola?

Once connected, you can ask Claude to retrieve meeting notes, summarise action items from specific meetings, search past transcripts, and use meeting context to draft follow-up emails, project briefs, or reports — all without leaving Claude.

Is my Granola meeting data shared with Claude automatically?

No. Claude only accesses your Granola data when you explicitly ask it to in a conversation. The connector grants permission for Claude to query Granola on your behalf, but data is fetched on-demand rather than synced continuously.

Can I disconnect Granola from Claude later?

Yes. Return to Customize > Connectors in Claude, find Granola in your connected integrations, and select the option to disconnect or revoke access. You can also revoke the connection from within your Granola account settings under connected apps.
