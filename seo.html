Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about the AI for SEO live training.

Is this a live in-person course?⌄

Yes — this is a live, in-person training at our Singapore office. You'll work through real SEO tasks with AI tools and get hands-on guidance from the trainer throughout.

Do I need to know how to code?⌄

No coding required at all. This course is designed for marketers and business owners — we use AI to handle the technical side of SEO, so you focus on strategy and results.

What experience level is this course for?⌄

The course is suitable for all levels — from beginners who want to learn SEO the AI-native way, to experienced SEOs looking to dramatically increase their output and efficiency.

What tools will I need access to?⌄

Bring your laptop. Access to Claude or ChatGPT (free tier is fine) and a Google Search Console account are helpful. All other tools are provided or demoed during the training.

How many people are in each session?⌄

Sessions are capped at 12 participants to ensure everyone gets practical time with the tools and personalised feedback on their own websites.
