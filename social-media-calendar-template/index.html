FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is built in Google Sheets — no extra software, scheduling tools, or paid plugins needed. Open it in your browser, fill in your content plan, and you have a full month of social media posts structured across all five platforms in one place.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use before your next monthly content planning session.

Who is this social media content calendar designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone who publishes content consistently — social media managers, content creators, freelancers, and business owners who want a structured, repeatable monthly planning system across Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, and X without juggling multiple tools or rebuilding their calendar from scratch every month.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

A fully structured content planning system across 8 dedicated sections:

  • Monthly content grid — clean 30-day grid with rows per platform for a full posting schedule at a glance
  • Post planning columns — date, platform, content format, caption draft, visual brief, hashtags, and publishing status per entry
  • Content pillar framework — built-in Educate, Inspire, Promote, and Engage breakdown to keep your feed balanced and strategic
  • Platform-specific sheets — dedicated tabs for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, and X with platform-native format guidance
  • Caption templates — fill-in-the-blank frameworks for 6 common post types including listicle, story, tip, question, announcement, and case study
  • Hashtag bank — organised section for branded, niche, and trending tags per platform
  • Content repurposing planner — map how one piece of content can be repurposed across all 5 platforms to multiply output
  • Monthly performance log — track reach, engagement, and clicks month by month to identify what content works best
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can type into Google Sheets, you can use this calendar. No integrations, no API connections, no scheduling software required — it's a plug-and-play planning system that works alongside whatever publishing tool you already use, whether that's Buffer, Later, Hootsuite, or native platform scheduling.

How does this actually save 6 hours per month?⌄

The time drain in social media management isn't the posting — it's the planning. Deciding what to create, which format to use, which platform to post on, and what to write each day adds up fast. This template eliminates all of that decision-making upfront. With a 30-day grid, content pillar framework, caption templates, and repurposing planner already built in, you sit down once at the start of the month, fill in your plan, and execute — no daily scrambling, no blank page paralysis.

Can I use this template for multiple clients or brands?⌄

Yes. Just duplicate the file for each client or brand, fill in their content pillars and posting schedule, and you have a professional, structured content plan ready to present every month. Freelancers and agencies managing multiple social media accounts will find it especially useful for onboarding new clients quickly, delivering consistent monthly content plans, and repurposing content across platforms to maximise output without increasing hours.
