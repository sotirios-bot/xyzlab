Common questions about the prompt library.

What AI tools do these prompts work with? ⌄

All prompts are tested with ChatGPT (GPT-4o) and Claude (Sonnet or Opus). They also work with Gemini and other large language models. No special syntax — just copy, fill in the blanks, and paste.

Do I need SEO experience to use this? ⌄

No. Every prompt has [PLACEHOLDER] fields you fill in with your own details before pasting. No prompt engineering needed. If you understand what your website is about and who your customers are, you can use these prompts.

What is AEO and why does it matter? ⌄

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation — the practice of optimising your content to appear in AI-generated answers (Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini). As more searches are answered directly by AI tools, AEO is becoming as important as traditional SEO. This library includes 6 dedicated AEO prompts covering AI Overviews, featured snippets, and topic authority.

How is this different from free prompt lists online? ⌄

Free prompt lists give you vague starting points like "write me an SEO title." These prompts tell the AI exactly what to do, what format to return, and what to include — so the first output is ready to use, not a rough draft you have to rewrite.
