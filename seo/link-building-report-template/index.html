FAQ

Link Building Report Template, FAQ

Common questions about the template.

What does this template track? ⌄

The template has two main tabs. The Outreach Tracker records every link building request you send — number sent, prospects replied, reply rate (auto-calculated) and outreach status per prospect. The Backlink Log records every link you win — number of backlinks won, domain authority, referring domain, target URL and anchor text.

Does this work in Google Sheets? ⌄

Yes. Upload the Excel file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. All formatting and columns carry over. You can then share it as a Google Sheets link with clients or teammates.

What's in the AI Prompts tab? ⌄

Five structured prompts: link building campaign summary, outreach response rate analysis, link quality assessment, client link building report narrative, and prospecting strategy suggestions. Each prompt has placeholder fields — add your data, paste into ChatGPT or Claude, and receive a written analysis in under 30 seconds.

Can I use this to report to clients? ⌄

Yes. The Link Acquisition tab gives you a clean summary of links won, domain authorities and anchor text distribution — exactly what clients want to see. Use the AI prompts tab to generate a written narrative from your data and paste it into your client report.

How do I get the template? ⌄

Submit the form above and you'll receive the free Excel template by email within a few minutes. No payment required. Check your inbox and spam folder if it doesn't arrive promptly.
