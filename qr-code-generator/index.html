Tips for better QR codes

🔗

Use a short URL

Shorter URLs create less dense QR codes that are easier to scan. Use your UTM Builder to create a clean tagged URL first.

🖨️

Test before printing

Always scan the QR code on your phone before printing or publishing. Test from multiple distances and in different lighting conditions.

📐

Minimum size 2.5cm

For printed materials, the QR code should be at least 2.5 × 2.5cm (roughly 300px digital) to scan reliably.

Frequently asked questions

Is this QR code generator really free?
Yes — completely free. No sign-up, no watermark, no limit on how many you generate.
What URL formats work?
Any URL works — including https://, http://, and deep links. For best results, use a full URL starting with https://. You can also encode plain text, phone numbers or email addresses.
What file format is the download?
PNG — a lossless image format that works for digital use and print. For high-resolution print materials, use the Large size (400px) and export at high DPI in your design tool.
