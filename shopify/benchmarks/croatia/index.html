FAQ

Shopify Benchmarks — Croatia FAQ

Common questions about Shopify store performance in Croatia.

What is a good conversion rate for Shopify in Croatia? ⌄

The average Shopify conversion rate in Croatia is around 2–3% across all industries. Fashion, beauty and health & wellness stores tend to perform above average due to strong impulse purchase behaviour. High-ticket categories like furniture and electronics typically see lower conversion rates of 0.5–1.5%. Optimising your product pages, checkout flow and payment options for local buyers can significantly lift your conversion rate.

What is a good ROAS for Shopify merchants in Croatia? ⌄

A blended ROAS of 2.5–4x is considered healthy for Shopify stores in Croatia across most industries. Stores with higher margins (beauty, digital products, subscriptions) can sustain lower ROAS targets, while low-margin categories like electronics need 5x+ to remain profitable. Always calculate your break-even ROAS based on your product margin before benchmarking against industry averages.

What is a typical cart abandonment rate in Croatia? ⌄

Cart abandonment rates for Shopify stores in Croatia typically range between 65–80%. The global average is around 70%, so rates above 80% indicate friction in your checkout — common causes include unexpected shipping costs, limited payment options, or a slow mobile checkout experience. Abandoned cart email flows and retargeting ads are the most effective recovery tactics for Croatia shoppers.

How does CPA differ across industries for Shopify in Croatia? ⌄

CPA varies widely by industry. Low-AOV categories like food & beverage or apparel can see CPAs as low as 10–20% of order value, while high-consideration purchases like furniture, automotive accessories and legal services can see CPAs that exceed the first-order value. Track CPA alongside LTV (lifetime value) to get a realistic view of profitability — especially for subscription and repeat-purchase businesses in Croatia.

How often are these Shopify benchmarks updated? ⌄

Benchmarks are reviewed and updated regularly based on anonymised merchant data and published industry reports for Croatia. Individual results will vary based on your product category, traffic sources, pricing strategy, and checkout optimisation. Use these figures as directional targets rather than exact thresholds.
