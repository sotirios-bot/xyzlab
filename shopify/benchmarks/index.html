What are Shopify benchmarks?

Shopify benchmarks are industry median performance figures — conversion rate, CPA, ROAS, cart abandonment rate and profit margin — aggregated across thousands of Shopify stores. They give you a reference point to evaluate whether your own store is performing above, at, or below industry norms in your market.

All figures are shown in local currency and adjusted for country-level market conditions. Shopify store benchmarks differ significantly from ad platform benchmarks because they reflect end-to-end store performance across all traffic sources — not just paid advertising.

Conv. Rate

Conversion Rate

Purchases ÷ sessions. The primary eCommerce health metric.

CPA

Cost Per Acquisition

Blended cost per customer acquired across all marketing channels.

ROAS

Return on Ad Spend

Revenue ÷ ad spend. Overall return on advertising investment.

Cart Abandonment

Cart Abandonment Rate

% of carts not completed. Average is ~70%; lower is better.

Profit Margin

Profit Margin

Net margin after COGS, shipping, returns and platform fees.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Shopify store benchmarks.

Why do Shopify conversion rates vary so much by country?⌄

Shopify conversion rates vary by country due to differences in eCommerce maturity, payment infrastructure, consumer trust and mobile internet penetration. Mature markets like the USA, UK and Australia have well-established digital payment habits and high consumer confidence in online shopping, producing higher conversion rates. Emerging markets in Latin America, Southeast Asia and Africa have rapidly growing eCommerce adoption but lower baseline conversion rates due to payment friction and trust barriers. Industry, product category and price point also cause significant variation within each market.

What is a good ROAS for a Shopify store?⌄

A good ROAS for a Shopify store is generally 3–5x across all industries, but the right target depends on your gross margin. Calculate your break-even ROAS by dividing 1 by your gross margin percentage — a 40% margin store breaks even at 2.5x ROAS. Track blended ROAS across all marketing channels (paid social, search, email, organic) for the most meaningful picture of advertising profitability.

What is a good cart abandonment rate for Shopify?⌄

The global average cart abandonment rate is approximately 70% — meaning 7 in 10 carts are never completed. A rate below 65% is considered strong for most Shopify categories. The biggest drivers of abandonment are unexpected shipping costs, forced account creation, slow checkout and limited payment options. Shopify's one-page checkout, Shop Pay, and a well-crafted 3-email abandoned cart sequence are the most effective tools for reducing abandonment.

How often are these Shopify benchmarks updated?⌄

Benchmarks are reviewed and updated quarterly. The current data reflects up-to-date industry aggregates compiled from anonymised store data and published industry reports. Individual results will vary based on product category, price point, marketing mix and store optimisation.
