FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is built in Google Sheets — no extra software, plugins, or paid tools needed. Just open it in your browser, paste in your campaign data, and your report is ready to go.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use on your next reporting cycle.

Who is this Google Ads report template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone who needs to report on paid search performance — PPC managers, Google Ads specialists, agency account managers, and in-house marketing teams who want to present campaign results clearly and professionally without spending hours on formatting.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

15+ pre-built KPIs across 8 fully structured report sections:

  • Campaign performance overview — impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, and ROAS in one view
  • Ad group breakdown — audience and theme-level performance to identify what's driving results and what's wasting budget
  • Keyword performance table — sortable data with Quality Scores, impression share, average position, and CPA
  • Search term analysis — dedicated tab for reviewing actual queries, flagging irrelevant terms, and identifying negative keyword opportunities
  • Month-on-month comparison — automatic trend highlighting without manual calculations
  • Budget pacing tracker — over- and under-pacing flagged automatically per campaign
  • Insights & recommendations section — pre-formatted commentary block for analysis and next steps
  • Executive summary tab — single-page client-facing view with headline numbers only
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can copy and paste data into Google Sheets, you can use this template. No formulas to build, no design work required — the structure, formatting, and calculations are already done for you.

How much time will this actually save me?⌄

The template is built to save around 3 hours per report. Instead of rebuilding your layout, writing comparison formulas, and formatting cells every month, you paste in your data and the report structures itself — leaving you to focus on the insights, not the spreadsheet.

Can I use this template across multiple client accounts?⌄

Yes. Just duplicate the sheet for each client, paste in their campaign data, and you have a consistent, professional report for every account. Agencies managing multiple Google Ads accounts will find it especially useful for standardising reporting quality across the board.
