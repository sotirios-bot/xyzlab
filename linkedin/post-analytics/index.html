FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about viewing post analytics on LinkedIn.

How do I see analytics for my LinkedIn posts?

Open your profile, go to the Activity section, click Show all posts, then click View analytics beneath any post. This shows impressions, engagement, and audience data for that individual post.

What metrics does LinkedIn post analytics show?

You'll see impressions (how many times the post was shown), members reached, and engagement such as reactions, comments, reposts, and clicks. For many posts you also get audience breakdowns like job titles, companies, locations, and industries.

Why don't I see "View analytics" under a post?

Analytics are only available on your own posts, and there can be a short delay after publishing before data appears. Very old posts created before LinkedIn rolled out analytics may also not show the link.

Can I see analytics on the LinkedIn mobile app?

Yes. Open your profile, scroll to Activity, tap Show all posts, and tap View analytics (or the bar-chart icon) under any post. The data matches what you see on desktop.

What's the difference between impressions and reach?

Impressions count every time your post is displayed, including repeat views by the same person. Members reached counts the number of unique people who saw it. A high impression-to-reach ratio means people are seeing your post more than once.

Can I export my LinkedIn post analytics?

Individual post analytics are mostly view-only, but if you manage a Company Page you can export content and post analytics to a spreadsheet from the page's Analytics tab. For personal profiles, your creator analytics dashboard offers a broader summary over time.
