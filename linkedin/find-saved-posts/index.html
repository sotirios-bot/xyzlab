FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about saved posts on LinkedIn.

Where do I find my saved posts on LinkedIn? ⌄

On desktop, go to your feed and click Saved items in the left-hand menu, then open Saved posts and articles. On mobile, tap your profile icon at the top-left, scroll down, and select Saved posts.

How do I save a post on LinkedIn? ⌄

Click the three dots (…) in the top-right corner of any post and choose Save. The post is added to your Saved items so you can come back to it later — the author is not notified when you save their post.

Can other people see what I've saved? ⌄

No. Your Saved items are private — only you can see the posts, articles, and jobs you've saved. Saving a post is not the same as liking or reposting it, so it never appears in your activity or your connections' feeds.

Is there a limit to how many posts I can save? ⌄

LinkedIn doesn't publish a hard cap, and in practice you can save a large number of posts and articles. They stay in your Saved items until you remove them, so it's a handy place to bookmark content you want to read or reference later.

How do I remove a saved post? ⌄

Open your Saved posts and articles list, find the post, click the three dots (…) or the bookmark/ribbon icon, and choose Unsave. It's removed from your saved list immediately.

What's the difference between saved posts and liked posts? ⌄

Saving is a private bookmark — nobody sees it and it doesn't interact with the post. Liking is a public reaction that can appear in your activity and your network's feed. If you want to revisit posts you reacted to instead, use the Reactions filter in your profile activity.
