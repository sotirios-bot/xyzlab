FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is built in Google Sheets — no extra software, plugins, or paid tools needed. Open it in your browser, paste in your channel data, and you have a complete multi-channel ecommerce report ready to share.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use on your next reporting cycle.

Who is this ecommerce report template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone who needs a complete picture of ecommerce performance across multiple channels — ecommerce managers, DTC brand owners, and growth marketers who want to connect Google Ads, Meta Ads, email, SEO, and organic data to revenue in one clean view, without pulling reports from five different platforms.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

A fully structured multi-channel report across 8 revenue-focused sections:

  • Revenue overview — total revenue broken down by channel with MoM and YoY comparisons
  • Channel attribution summary — which channels are driving the most orders and revenue, and how attribution has shifted over the period
  • ROAS by channel — return on ad spend across Google, Meta, TikTok, and email so you know where every dollar is working hardest
  • Conversion funnel analysis — sessions to product views, add to cart, checkout initiation, and purchase with drop-off rates at each stage
  • Top products by revenue — best-selling products ranked by revenue, units sold, and conversion rate with trend indicators
  • Abandoned cart metrics — cart abandonment rate, recovery rate, and revenue recovered through email and retargeting flows
  • Customer acquisition cost — CAC by channel and blended CAC tracked over time to monitor efficiency as you scale
  • Lifetime value snapshot — average order value, purchase frequency, and estimated LTV to contextualise acquisition spending
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can export data from Google Ads, Meta Ads Manager, your email platform, and Google Analytics, and paste it into Google Sheets, you can use this template. No formula-building, no design work — the structure, calculations, and formatting are already built in.

How is this different from a single-channel ads report template?⌄

Most report templates are built around one platform. This one is built around revenue — connecting paid search, paid social, email marketing, SEO, and direct traffic into a single view so you can see how every channel contributes to total ecommerce performance. ROAS, CAC, LTV, and conversion funnel data all sit in one place, giving you the full picture instead of isolated channel snapshots.

Can I use this template across multiple stores or client accounts?⌄

Yes. Just duplicate the sheet for each brand or store, paste in their channel data, and you have a consistent, professional ecommerce report every time. Whether you're managing one DTC brand or a portfolio of online stores, the template scales with you — bringing all your revenue data together in a format that's clear for internal teams and impressive for stakeholders.
