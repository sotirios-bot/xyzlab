Who is this Google Ads audit checklist designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone managing or auditing Google Ads accounts — PPC managers, Google Ads agencies, and business owners who want a comprehensive, structured process for finding wasted spend, fixing underperforming campaigns, and identifying growth opportunities without missing a single account area.

What does the checklist actually cover?⌄

60+ checks across 8 core Google Ads audit areas:

  • Account structure & settings — campaign naming, account organisation, time zone, currency, auto-applied recommendations settings, and linked accounts
  • Campaign configuration — budget allocation, bidding strategy alignment, network settings, geo targeting, ad scheduling, and device modifiers
  • Ad copy & creative quality — RSA ad strength scores, headline variety, keyword pinning usage, and CTA clarity across all active ad groups
  • Bidding strategy review — Smart Bidding eligibility, target CPA and ROAS settings, impression share benchmarks, and portfolio bid strategy performance

  • Ad extensions & assets — all eligible asset types reviewed including sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, call, lead form, and image assets
  • Conversion tracking verification — all conversions firing correctly, no duplicates, correct attribution windows, and GA4 linking confirmed
