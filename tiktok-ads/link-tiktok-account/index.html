Connect TikTok Account to TikTok Ads Manager

Watch the step-by-step walkthrough below, then follow along to link a TikTok account to your TikTok Ads Manager via Business Center — using the QR code flow to get the account owner's approval.

Step by Step

Connect TikTok Account to TikTok Ads Manager

Follow these steps alongside the video to link a TikTok organic account to your Business Center so you can use it in ad campaigns.

1

Login to TikTok Business Center

  • Go to business.tiktok.com and log in with your TikTok Business Center account
2

Navigate to Accounts (left-hand menu)

  • In the left-hand navigation sidebar, click on Accounts
3

Select TikTok Accounts

  • Under Accounts, click TikTok Accounts to manage linked organic TikTok profiles
4

Request access to run ad campaigns

  • Click Request Access to initiate the connection process for a TikTok account you do not own
5

Generate the QR Code and share it with the TikTok account owner

  • TikTok will generate a QR code that the account owner must scan to grant access
  • Share the QR code with the TikTok account owner (screenshot, screen share, or messaging app)
  • The QR code is time-limited — the owner should scan it promptly
6

TikTok account owner scans the QR code and accepts

  • The TikTok account owner opens the TikTok app on their phone
  • They tap their Profile icon, then go to Find Friends
  • They tap the QR code scanner icon and scan the code you shared
  • They review and accept the access request to authorise your Business Center
7

Navigate back to Accounts in TikTok Business Center

  • Once the owner has accepted, return to TikTok Business Center → Accounts
8

Click on Add Assets

  • Click the Add Assets button to assign the newly linked TikTok account to your ad account
9

Select TikTok Accounts

  • In the asset type selection, choose TikTok Accounts
10

Check the TikTok account and save

  • Tick the checkbox next to the TikTok account you just linked
  • Click Save — the account is now connected and available to use in your TikTok Ads Manager campaigns

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about connecting TikTok accounts to TikTok Ads Manager.

Why do I need to connect a TikTok account to run ads? ⌄

Connecting a TikTok organic account to your Ads Manager allows you to run Spark Ads — a TikTok ad format that boosts existing organic posts or other creators' content. This is important for brands and agencies running In-Feed ads that appear native to the platform. Without a linked TikTok account, you can still run standard non-Spark ads, but you won't be able to use your organic content or the account's identity in paid campaigns.

What is the difference between "Request Access" and linking my own account? ⌄

Request Access is used when you are an agency or advertiser who wants to connect a TikTok account you do not own (e.g. a client's brand account). The account owner must approve the request via QR code. If you own the TikTok account yourself and are logged in with the same credentials, TikTok may allow a more direct linking flow. The QR code method is the most common approach for agencies connecting client accounts.

The QR code expired before the account owner could scan it — what should I do? ⌄

Simply repeat the Request Access flow to generate a new QR code. TikTok's QR codes are time-limited for security reasons. If the owner is not immediately available, consider screenshotting the QR code and sending it via a messaging app — but make sure they scan it quickly. Coordinate with the account owner before starting the flow so they are ready to scan as soon as you generate the code.

Can I connect multiple TikTok accounts to one Business Center? ⌄

Yes. You can connect multiple TikTok organic accounts to a single Business Center. This is useful for agencies managing several client brands — each brand's TikTok account can be linked and then assigned to the relevant ad account within the Business Center. When creating a campaign, you can select which connected TikTok account to use as the identity for your ads.

What access does my Business Center get after connecting a TikTok account? ⌄

After the account owner approves the request, your Business Center gains advertising access to use that TikTok account in campaigns — specifically to run Spark Ads using the account's posts. You do not get access to post content, respond to comments, or manage the organic profile. The account owner retains full control of their TikTok account and can revoke your access at any time from their TikTok app settings.

How do I disconnect a TikTok account from Business Center? ⌄

Go to TikTok Business Center → Accounts → TikTok Accounts, find the account you want to remove, and click the options menu to disconnect it. Note that the TikTok account owner can also revoke access independently from within the TikTok app (Settings → Privacy → Authorised Apps). Once disconnected, the account will no longer be available to use in new ad campaigns, though existing campaigns using that account's identity may be affected.
