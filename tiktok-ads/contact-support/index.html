Step by Step

Contact TikTok Ads Support

Follow these steps alongside the video to reach TikTok Ads customer support via live chat.

1

Login to TikTok Ads Manager

2

Hover over the ? icon on the top-right-hand side

  • The help icon is located in the top navigation bar, next to your account settings
3

Select Customer support

4

Click on Get support

5

Select the topic that matches your question

  • Choose the category that best describes your issue — such as Account, Campaign, Billing, or Pixel & Events
6

Describe the issue and click on Enter chat

  • Provide as much detail as possible — include your Ad Account ID, campaign name, and any error messages you have seen
  • A TikTok support agent will join the chat to assist you

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about contacting TikTok Ads support.

How do I contact TikTok Ads support?

The fastest way to contact TikTok Ads support is through the live chat feature built into Ads Manager. Click the ? icon in the top-right corner, select Customer Support, then click Get Support. Choose a topic, describe your issue, and click Enter Chat to connect with a support agent directly.

Is TikTok Ads support available 24/7?

TikTok Ads live chat support is generally available during business hours depending on your region, but availability may vary. If no agents are available when you initiate a chat, you may be offered the option to leave a message or submit a support ticket, which will be followed up via email.

What issues can TikTok Ads support help me with?

TikTok Ads support can help with a wide range of issues including account access and verification, campaign and ad rejection appeals, billing and payment problems, Pixel and event tracking setup, audience creation, and general platform navigation. For complex campaign strategy questions, consider booking a coaching session instead.

What should I prepare before contacting TikTok Ads support?

To get faster resolution, have the following ready before starting a chat: your Ad Account ID (found in the top-left corner of Ads Manager), the campaign or ad name you need help with, any error messages or rejection reasons you received, and screenshots of the issue where possible. The more context you provide upfront, the quicker support can diagnose the problem.

Can I contact TikTok Ads support by email or phone?

TikTok Ads does not offer a public phone support line. The primary support channel is live chat within Ads Manager. If the chat is unavailable, you can submit a help ticket through the support portal and expect a follow-up by email. Some enterprise or high-spend accounts may have access to a dedicated account manager for more direct support.

How long does it take for TikTok Ads support to respond?

Live chat responses are typically near-instant during operating hours, though wait times can vary based on demand. If you submit a support ticket outside chat hours, email responses generally arrive within 1–3 business days. For urgent issues such as account suspensions or billing errors, using the live chat during peak hours is the fastest option.
