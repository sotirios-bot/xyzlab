Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about this skill.

What GA4 data do I need to share?⌄

Export your standard GA4 performance report — sessions, users, channels, conversions, and key pages. Any export format works.

Can it analyse specific pages or landing pages?⌄

Yes. Include page-level data in your export and tell the skill which pages to focus on.

Does it handle eCommerce data?⌄

For eCommerce-specific analysis (revenue, AOV, product performance), use the eCommerce Analysis Skill instead — it's built specifically for that.

How often should I use this skill?⌄

Monthly for most businesses. For high-traffic sites or active campaigns, weekly reporting is common.
