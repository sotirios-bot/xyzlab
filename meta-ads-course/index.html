Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about the AI for Meta Ads live training.

Is this a live in-person course?⌄

Yes — this is a live, in-person training held at our Singapore office. You'll work on real Meta campaigns and get direct feedback from the trainer throughout the day.

Do I need a Facebook Business account?⌄

Having an existing Meta Business Manager account is helpful but not required. We'll walk through live campaign setups and you can apply everything to your own account right away.

What experience level is this course for?⌄

Suitable for all levels — whether you're just getting started with Meta Ads or you're an experienced social media buyer looking to integrate AI for better results and efficiency.

What do I need to bring?⌄

Bring your laptop. All course materials, AI tool access, and templates are provided. You'll leave with a full creative and audience framework ready to deploy.

How many people are in each session?⌄

Sessions are capped at 12 participants so every attendee gets individual attention and hands-on practice time with the trainer.

When is the next session?⌄
