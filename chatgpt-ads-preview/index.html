Tips for ChatGPT sponsored placements

🎯

Lead with your strongest value prop

Users are in a task-focused mindset — your headline has less than a second to earn attention.

📏

Keep body text under 100 chars

Concise, specific copy tends to outperform in conversational contexts. Say more with less.

🔗

Use a clear, specific CTA

"Get a free quote", "See pricing" or "Start free" outperform generic "Learn more" in intent-driven placements.

Frequently asked questions

What are the official ChatGPT Ads specs?
OpenAI has not published full technical specifications for ChatGPT Ads publicly. This tool uses specs based on publicly reported information and may be updated as more details become available.
Who can run ChatGPT Ads?
ChatGPT Ads is currently in limited rollout. Access is being granted to select advertisers through OpenAI's early access programme. Check openai.com for the latest availability updates.
What makes a good ChatGPT sponsored placement?
Focus on relevance to the user's query. Unlike social ads, users in ChatGPT are actively seeking information — match your ad to their intent and lead with a clear, specific benefit.
