What's Covered

Every On-Page SEO Element, Covered

A step-by-step checklist that walks you through every on-page factor that influences how Google reads, indexes, and ranks your pages.

✓
Meta Tags & Title Optimisation

Title tag length, keyword placement, meta description character count, and uniqueness checks for every page.

✓
Heading Structure (H1–H4)

Verify your heading hierarchy is logical, keyword-informed, and accessible — with checks for duplicate H1s and missing headings.

✓
Keyword Placement & Density

Primary keyword in the right places — title, H1, intro paragraph, URL, and image alt text — without over-optimisation.

✓
Internal Linking Strategy

Check for descriptive anchor text, orphan pages, and opportunities to pass authority to priority pages through internal links.

✓
Image Optimisation & Alt Text

File name, alt text, compression, and format checks — so every image on the page contributes to SEO rather than hurting load speed.

✓
Schema Markup & Structured Data

Verify the right schema types are applied and validated — FAQ, Article, Product, BreadcrumbList, and more.

✓
Page Speed & Core Web Vitals

LCP, CLS, and INP checks with recommended thresholds — so your pages pass Google's performance requirements before going live.

✓
Mobile Usability & UX

Mobile viewport, tap target sizes, font readability, and CTA placement — so your pages work perfectly on every device.
