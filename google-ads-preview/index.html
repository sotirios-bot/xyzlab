Tips for stronger Google Ads copy

🎯

Use all 3 headlines

Google rotates combinations; more variety gives the algorithm better signals to find the highest-performing mix.

✍️

Include your keyword in Headline 1

Matching the search query in your first headline improves Ad Strength and Quality Score — and CTR.

📐

Stay within character limits

Truncated ads perform worse. Test your copy within the limits before uploading to Campaign Manager.

Frequently asked questions

How many headlines and descriptions does Google show?
Google shows up to 3 headlines and 2 descriptions at a time, selected from your pool of up to 15 headlines and 4 descriptions. This tool previews one combination.
Does the display URL have to match the final URL?
No. The display URL is what users see in the ad. It just needs to reflect your website domain. The final URL is where users actually land.
What's a good Ad Strength score?
Aim for "Excellent". Add unique, varied headlines and descriptions, include your keywords naturally, and avoid repeating the same phrases across fields.
