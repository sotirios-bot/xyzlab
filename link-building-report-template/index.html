FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is delivered as a downloadable spreadsheet file — compatible with Google Sheets and ready to import data directly from Ahrefs and Semrush. No extra software or plugins needed. Open it, paste in your link data, and your full link building pipeline is organised in one place.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use on your next link building campaign or client reporting cycle.

Who is this link building template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone managing backlink acquisition and outreach — SEO link builders, agency account managers, and content and digital PR teams who need a complete view of their link building pipeline from prospect outreach all the way through to live links, lost link monitoring, and client reporting.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

A fully structured link building tracker and report across 8 dedicated sections:

  • Link acquisition summary — new links built, total live links, and links lost this month with MoM comparison and net link growth calculation
  • Prospect tracking table — log every outreach prospect with status, DR, target URL, and contact details so nothing falls through the cracks
  • Domain authority & DR tracker — monitor average DR of acquired links over time to ensure your backlink profile is trending in the right direction
  • Anchor text distribution — track exact, partial match, branded, and naked URL anchors to keep your link profile natural and penalty-resistant
  • Live vs. lost link monitor — automatic flags for recently lost links so you can act before rankings are affected
  • Outreach response rate tracker — log emails sent, responses received, and conversion to live link to measure and improve outreach effectiveness
  • Campaign ROI calculation — connect link building activity to organic traffic and ranking improvements to prove the value of every link
  • Monthly executive summary — clean one-page client-facing view showing new links, DR trends, and next month's priorities
Does this template work with Ahrefs and Semrush data?⌄

Yes. The template is built to accept direct data imports from both Ahrefs and Semrush — the most widely used link building and backlink analysis tools in SEO. No reformatting or manual data entry required to get your link profile data into the tracker.

Can this template handle both outreach prospecting and live link reporting in one place?⌄

Yes — that's exactly what makes it different from a basic backlink tracker. Most spreadsheets track either prospects or live links. This template covers the full link building funnel — from initial prospect logging and outreach status tracking through to live link monitoring, lost link alerts, and client-ready monthly reporting, all in one file.

Can I use this template across multiple clients or link building campaigns?⌄

Absolutely. Just duplicate the file for each client or campaign, import their backlink data, and you have a consistent, professional link building report every time. Agency teams managing multiple SEO accounts will find it especially useful for standardising pipeline visibility, proving link building ROI to clients, and keeping outreach campaigns organised across the entire team — without anything getting lost in scattered spreadsheets.
