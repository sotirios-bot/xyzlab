Common questions about the prompt library.

What AI tools do these prompts work with? ⌄

All prompts are tested with ChatGPT (GPT-4o) and Claude (Sonnet or Opus). They also work with Gemini and other large language models. No special syntax — just copy, fill in the blanks, and paste.

No. Every prompt has [PLACEHOLDER] fields you fill in with your own details before pasting. Whether you're setting up GA4 for the first time or troubleshooting a tracking issue, these prompts guide the AI to give you a clear, actionable answer.

What's included in the full library? ⌄

50 prompts across 8 categories: Setup & Configuration (6), Events & Tracking (6), Audience & Users (6), Traffic & Acquisition (6), Conversions & Goals (7), Ecommerce (6), Reports & Dashboards (6), Troubleshooting (7). Delivered as a PDF and Google Doc so you can copy prompts directly.
