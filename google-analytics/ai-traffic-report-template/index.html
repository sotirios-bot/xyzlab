FAQ

AI Traffic Report Template, FAQ

Common questions about the template.

Does this work in Google Sheets? ⌄

Yes. Upload the Excel file to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets. All formulas, charts and formatting carry over. You can then share it as a Google Sheets link with clients or teammates.

What's in the AI Prompts tab? ⌄

Six structured prompts focused on AI traffic analysis: weekly AI traffic summary, monthly growth analysis, conversion quality by AI source, AEO content strategy, AI traffic client report, and competitor AI visibility tactics. Each prompt has placeholder fields — add your numbers, paste into ChatGPT or Claude, and receive a written analysis and action plan in under 30 seconds.

How do I get AI referral data from GA4? ⌄

In GA4, go to Reports > Acquisition > Traffic Acquisition and filter by Session Source. The referral domains to look for are chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai, gemini.google.com, and claude.ai. Copy the session data for each source into the template weekly and the calculations and charts will update automatically.

What is AEO and why does AI traffic matter? ⌄

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimisation — the practice of getting your site cited in AI-generated answers. ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini now drive significant referral traffic for many sites, and this channel is growing fast. This template helps you track where that traffic comes from, how it converts, and identify opportunities to grow your visibility in AI search results.

How do I receive the template? ⌄

Submit the form and you'll receive the free Excel template by email within a few minutes. No payment required. Check your inbox and spam folder if it doesn't arrive promptly.
