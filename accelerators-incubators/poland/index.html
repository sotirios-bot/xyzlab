FAQ

Common questions about the Accelerator & Incubator Database

What exactly is included in the database? ⌄

The database is an Excel/Google Sheets spreadsheet with 1,900+ programme records. Each record includes: tier (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3), investment stages accepted, who the programme is designed for, whether an idea or MVP is required, focus industries, regions covered, verified email contacts, and a direct application link URL.

What is the difference between an accelerator and an incubator? ⌄

Accelerators run fixed-term cohort programmes (typically 3–6 months) that take a small equity stake in exchange for funding, mentorship, and network access. Incubators are longer-term and more flexible — they provide workspace and support without a fixed timeline and don't always take equity. Both are included in this database and clearly labelled so you can apply to the right fit.

How recent is the data? ⌄

The database is updated quarterly. Application links and email contacts are verified before each update to minimise dead ends. You'll receive the most current version, and future updates are included for 12 months after purchase.

What file format will I receive? ⌄

You'll receive an Excel (.xlsx) file that also works in Google Sheets. Every column is filter-ready so you can narrow down programmes by tier, stage, focus, or region and build a targeted shortlist in minutes — no manual research required.

Do I need an idea or MVP to apply to programmes in the database? ⌄

It varies — and that's exactly why we included the "Idea needed?" column. Some programmes accept founders at the pre-idea stage; others require a working product or early traction. Each record clearly indicates the minimum requirement so you only spend time on applications you actually qualify for.
