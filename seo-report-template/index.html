FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is built in Google Sheets — no extra software, plugins, or paid tools needed. Just open it in your browser, paste in your organic data, and your report is ready to present.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use before your next client reporting cycle.

Who is this SEO report template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone who needs to report on organic search performance — SEO specialists, agency account managers, and in-house marketing teams who want to present keyword rankings, traffic growth, and technical progress clearly and professionally without spending hours in spreadsheets.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

12+ pre-built metrics across 8 fully structured report sections:

  • Organic traffic overview — sessions, users, and pageviews with month-on-month and year-on-year comparisons in one clean view
  • Keyword ranking tracker — position tracking over time across priority terms with automatic rank change indicators
  • Position change highlights — biggest keyword movers up and down, surfaced automatically so you can talk to the story behind the data
  • Backlink growth summary — new links acquired, referring domains, and domain authority growth over the reporting period
  • Core Web Vitals snapshot — LCP, CLS, and INP scores with pass/fail flags and improvement notes
  • Top pages by organic sessions — highest-traffic pages identified, plus pages that have dropped and need attention
  • Technical issue log — pre-formatted table for crawl errors, redirect issues, and outstanding or completed fixes
  • Executive summary tab — single-page client-facing view showing only headline wins and next month's priorities
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can export data from Google Search Console or GA4 and paste it into Google Sheets, you can use this template. No formula-building, no design work — the structure, formatting, and calculations are already built in.

How much time will this actually save me?⌄

The template is built to save around 2 hours per report. Instead of rebuilding your layout, manually calculating rank changes, and formatting sections from scratch every month, you paste in your data and the report structures itself — leaving you to focus on the commentary, not the spreadsheet.

Can I use this template for both monthly and quarterly reporting?⌄

Yes. The template includes both monthly and quarterly report formats, so you can use it for regular client updates and for deeper performance reviews without needing a separate document. Duplicate the sheet per client and you have a consistent, professional reporting system across your entire SEO portfolio.
