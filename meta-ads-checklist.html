What's Covered

Every Meta Ads Setup and Audit Area, Covered

From Business Manager setup to ongoing optimisation — this checklist covers everything you need to launch and manage Meta campaigns with confidence.

✓
Business Manager & Account Setup

Business Manager structure, ad account settings, user roles, payment method, and account spending limits all verified before launch.

✓
Meta Pixel & Conversion Events

Pixel firing on all key pages, conversion events configured in Events Manager, and Conversions API set up for improved signal quality.

✓
Campaign Structure & Objectives

Campaign objective matches business goal, funnel stage logic applied, and budget allocation reviewed across prospecting and retargeting.

✓
Audience Setup & Targeting

Audience size in the recommended range, custom audiences created from pixel data, and lookalikes based on your best converters.

✓
Creative Specs & Ad Formats

Image and video specs correct for every placement — Feed, Stories, Reels, and Audience Network — with correct aspect ratios verified.

✓
Copy, CTA & Landing Page Review

Primary text, headline, and CTA reviewed for clarity and relevance — and landing page checked for speed, mobile UX, and message match.

✓
Budget, Bidding & Schedule

Budget type (daily vs. lifetime), bidding strategy, bid cap settings, and ad schedule reviewed before campaigns go live.

✓
Ongoing Optimisation Checklist

Weekly and monthly checks — frequency monitoring, creative fatigue detection, audience overlap review, and ROAS threshold alerts.
