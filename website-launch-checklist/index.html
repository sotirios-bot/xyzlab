What's Covered

Every Pre-Launch Check, Covered

A step-by-step checklist that walks you through every critical area before your website goes live — so nothing important gets missed.

✓
SEO Foundations

title tags, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, XML sitemap, robots.txt, and Google Search Console verification before anything goes live.

✓
Analytics & Tracking Setup

GA4 installation, goal configuration, conversion tracking, GTM container deployment, and cross-domain tracking if needed.

✓
Performance & Core Web Vitals

LCP, CLS, and INP checks, image compression, lazy loading, caching rules, and CDN configuration for fast load times.

✓
Forms & Conversion Points

test every form submission, confirmation email, redirect, and thank-you page — including mobile submission flows.

✓
Mobile & Cross-Browser Testing

verify layout, navigation, CTAs, and font rendering across Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and major mobile devices.

✓
Security & HTTPS

SSL certificate active, HTTP→HTTPS redirects set, mixed content warnings resolved, and security headers configured.

✓
Content & Copy Review

proofread every page for typos, broken links (internal and external), placeholder text removed, and images with missing alt text.

✓
Legal & Compliance

privacy policy in place, cookie consent banner configured, GDPR/PDPA requirements met, and terms of service linked in the footer.
