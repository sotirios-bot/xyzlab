FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the checklist come in?⌄

The checklist is delivered as a downloadable file — clearly structured, agency-tested, and ready to use straight away. No technical setup required. Open it, work through each of the 8 categories, and every critical pre-launch element is covered before your website goes live.

How do I receive the checklist after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use on your next website launch or pre-live review.

Who is this website launch checklist designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone who launches websites — web developers and agencies who need a standard pre-handoff QA process, founders and business owners who want to go live with confidence, and digital marketers who need to verify that SEO foundations, analytics, and conversion tracking are all in place before driving traffic to a new site.

What does the checklist actually cover?⌄

60+ checks across 8 critical pre-launch categories:

  • SEO foundations — title tags, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, XML sitemap submission, robots.txt, and Google Search Console verification
  • Analytics & tracking setup — GA4 installation, goal configuration, conversion tracking, GTM container deployment, and cross-domain tracking
  • Performance & Core Web Vitals — LCP, CLS, and INP checks, image compression, lazy loading, caching rules, and CDN configuration
  • Forms & conversion points — every form submission, confirmation email, redirect, and thank-you page tested including mobile submission flows
  • Mobile & cross-browser testing — layout, navigation, CTAs, and font rendering verified across Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and major mobile devices
  • Security & HTTPS — SSL certificate active, HTTP to HTTPS redirects set, mixed content warnings resolved, and security headers configured
  • Content & copy review — proofread for typos, broken internal and external links, placeholder text removed, and missing image alt text flagged
  • Legal & compliance — privacy policy, cookie consent banner, GDPR and PDPA requirements, and terms of service all verified
Do I need technical web development experience to use this checklist?⌄

No. Each check is clearly explained so you know exactly what to verify and why it matters — whether you're a developer running a structured client handoff process or a business owner launching your own website independently. The checklist covers both technical and non-technical areas, so every type of user can work through it confidently without missing anything critical.

Can I use this checklist for every website launch, or is it built for a specific platform?⌄

It's fully platform-agnostic — it works for WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Squarespace, custom builds, and any other CMS or website platform. The checks cover universal pre-launch requirements that apply regardless of how your site was built, making it a reliable standard process for every project you launch.

Can I use this checklist across multiple client projects?⌄

Absolutely. Work through the checklist on every website before handoff or go-live, document what's passing and what still needs attention, and you have a clear audit trail for every project. Web agencies and developers will find it especially useful for standardising their pre-launch QA process across every client build — catching SEO gaps, tracking misconfigurations, and compliance issues before they become post-launch problems that damage rankings, waste ad spend, or expose the business to legal risk.
