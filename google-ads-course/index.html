Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about the AI for Google Ads live training.

Is this a live in-person course?⌄

Yes — this is a live, in-person training. You get hands-on practice with real campaigns and direct feedback from the trainer. No pre-recorded videos.

Do I need an active Google Ads account?⌄

Having an active account is helpful but not required. We'll use live campaign examples throughout the training, and you can apply everything to your own account immediately after.

What experience level is this course for?⌄

The course is designed for all levels — from marketers who have run a few campaigns to experienced PPC managers looking to integrate AI into their workflow. We cover foundations before diving into advanced AI techniques.

What do I need to bring?⌄

Just bring your laptop and a willingness to learn. All training materials, templates, and access to AI tools are provided during the session. You'll leave with a complete resource pack.

How many people are in each session?⌄

Sessions are kept small (max 12 participants) to ensure everyone gets individual attention and the trainer can address your specific account challenges.
