FAQ

Your Questions Answered

What format does the template come in?⌄

The template is built natively for GA4 and delivered as a Google Sheets file — no extra software, plugins, or paid tools needed. Open it in your browser, paste in your GA4 export data, and your report is ready to share. A shareable PDF format is also included for client-ready presentation.

How do I receive the template after purchase?⌄

It's sent directly to your inbox via email as soon as your purchase is confirmed. No account setup, no waiting — instant access, ready to use on your next reporting cycle.

Who is this GA4 report template designed for?⌄

It's built for anyone who needs to report on website analytics performance — marketing managers, agency analysts, and ecommerce teams who want to turn raw GA4 data into clear, meaningful insights without spending hours manually pulling reports from the interface.

What's actually included in the template?⌄

10+ pre-built KPIs across 8 fully structured report sections:

  • Traffic overview — sessions, users, new vs. returning visitors, and engagement metrics in one top-level view
  • Acquisition breakdown — channel-by-channel traffic split showing which sources are driving sessions, users, and conversions
  • Engagement & retention metrics — engaged sessions, engagement rate, average session duration, and pages per session over time
  • Conversion & goal summary — all key conversion events with conversion rate, total completions, and value tracked by channel
  • Top pages by revenue/goal — highest-contributing pages identified with traffic and conversion rate by page
  • Audience demographics — age, gender, and location breakdowns showing who is actually visiting and converting
  • Channel comparison MoM — automatic month-on-month change highlights by channel for instant trend and anomaly spotting
  • Event tracking summary — pre-formatted tab for key GA4 events including scroll depth, button clicks, and form submissions
Do I need any technical skills or special tools to use this template?⌄

None. If you can export data from GA4 and paste it into Google Sheets, you can use this template. All calculations are auto-built — no manual formulas, no design work, no GA4 expertise required to produce a polished report.

How much time will this actually save me?⌄

Significantly. Instead of manually pulling metrics from multiple GA4 reports, reformatting data, and building charts from scratch every month, you paste in your export and the template does the heavy lifting — leaving you to focus on the insights and recommendations, not the spreadsheet.

Can I use this template across multiple clients or websites?⌄

Yes. Just duplicate the sheet for each client or property, paste in their GA4 data, and you have a consistent, professional analytics report every time. Agency analysts managing multiple accounts will find it especially useful for standardising report quality and cutting down turnaround time across the entire portfolio.
