FAQ

Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about this skill.

What data do I need to share?⌄

Your Meta Ads campaign and ad set performance data for the reporting period — export directly from Ads Manager or paste from a spreadsheet.

Can it include period-over-period comparisons?⌄

Yes. Include data from the comparison period (e.g., last month) and the skill will automatically add period-over-period analysis.

Is the report suitable for clients?⌄

Yes — written in plain English suitable for clients, executives, or stakeholders at any level of Meta Ads knowledge.

Can I include creative performance in the report?⌄

Absolutely. Include ad-level creative data and the skill will add a creative performance section to the report.
