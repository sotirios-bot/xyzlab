Tips for better Meta Ads copy

🖼️

Hook in the first line

Only the first 125 chars of primary text show before "See more". Lead with your value proposition, not context.

📐

Test multiple placements

Your headline may be cut off on mobile or missing on Stories. Preview each placement before publishing.

🎯

Match headline to landing page

Consistent messaging between ad headline and landing page headline improves conversion rate.

Frequently asked questions

What's the primary text character limit on Meta Ads?
125 characters are shown before "See more" on most placements. You can write more, but only the first 125 show without a click.
Do all placements show the headline?
Not always. Facebook Feed and Instagram Feed show the headline below the image. Stories show only the CTA button overlay. Always check each placement.
What image size should I use?
1200 × 628 pixels (1.91:1) for Facebook Feed and 1080 × 1080 (1:1) for Instagram Feed. Stories use 1080 × 1920 (9:16). Using a square image (1:1) works across most placements.
