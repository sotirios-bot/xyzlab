What should a Google Ads report include?

A useful Google Ads report answers one question: are we hitting our goals, and if not, why not? To answer that, every report should include:

  • Spend vs budget — how much was spent against what was available
  • Conversion volume and cost per conversion (CPA) — the core business metric
  • Return on ad spend (ROAS) — especially for ecommerce campaigns
  • Click-through rate (CTR) — a signal of ad relevance and audience targeting quality
  • Quality Scores — leading indicator of future CPC and ad rank
  • Week-on-week or month-on-month change — context is everything in performance reporting

Avoid padding reports with impressions and average position data unless the client specifically asks for it. Focus on the numbers that connect directly to business outcomes.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Google Ads report template really free?
Yes — completely free. Enter your email to download it. You'll also receive the XYZ Lab newsletter, The Walkthrough, which you can unsubscribe from at any time.
Does it work with Microsoft Excel or just Google Sheets?
The template is compatible with both Google Sheets and Microsoft Excel. We recommend Google Sheets for the easiest sharing with clients, but all formulas are standard and work in Excel without modification.
How often should I send a Google Ads report to clients?
Monthly reporting is the standard for most clients. High-spend accounts (above $10K/month) or clients with an active optimisation schedule often warrant weekly check-ins using a simplified view of the same data.
Can I use this template for multiple clients?
Yes. Make a copy of the template in Google Drive for each client. Rename it with the client name and reporting period. There's no restriction on how many times you use it.
Do I need to connect any APIs or data sources?
No API connection is required. The template works with manually exported CSV data from Google Ads. If you want automated data pulling, you'll need a separate connector tool like Supermetrics or Google Ads' own Google Sheets integration.
What's the difference between this and a paid reporting tool?
Paid tools like Looker Studio, Supermetrics or Agency Analytics automate data pulling and refresh. This template is manual — you paste in your data. That makes it slower to update but free, portable, and fully customisable with no recurring subscription.
