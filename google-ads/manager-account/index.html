Create Google Ads Manager Account (Google Ads MCC Account)

Watch the step-by-step walkthrough below, then follow along with the guide to create your Google Ads Manager Account (MCC) so you can manage multiple ad accounts from one central dashboard.

Step by Step

Create Google Ads Manager Account (Google Ads MCC Account)

Follow these steps alongside the video to set up your Manager Account.

1

Go to the Google Ads Manager Accounts page

  • Navigate to ads.google.com/home/tools/manager-accounts/ in your browser
  • Make sure you are signed in with the Google account you want to use for the MCC
2

Click the "Go to manager accounts" button to get started

  • This button takes you into the Manager Account creation flow
  • If you already have a Manager Account linked to this Google profile, you will be taken directly to the MCC dashboard instead
3

Fill in the account details to complete setup

  • Enter your account name (this is internal — your clients won't see it)
  • Select your primary use: managing my own ads, or managing ads for other businesses
  • Choose your country, time zone, and currency
  • Accept the Terms of Service and click Submit
  • Your new Manager Account is now live — you can link existing Google Ads accounts or create new ones from the MCC dashboard

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Google Ads Manager Accounts (MCC).

What is a Google Ads Manager Account (MCC)? ⌄

A Manager Account (formerly called My Client Center, or MCC) is a Google Ads account that lets you manage multiple individual Google Ads accounts from a single login. It is the standard account type used by agencies, freelancers, and businesses that run ads across multiple brands or clients. A Manager Account does not run ads itself — it simply acts as a hub to access and manage the accounts linked beneath it.

Do I need a Manager Account if I only have one Google Ads account? ⌄

Not necessarily — a single-account advertiser can manage everything directly from a standard Google Ads account. However, a Manager Account is useful even with one account if you want to use Google Ads Editor with multiple accounts, access cross-account reporting, or plan to manage additional accounts in the future. Agencies almost always use an MCC.

Is a Google Ads Manager Account free to create? ⌄

Yes. Creating a Manager Account is completely free. You only pay for ad spend within the individual Google Ads accounts linked to your MCC — the MCC itself has no cost. There is also no minimum spend or account requirement to open one.

What is the difference between a Manager Account and a standard Google Ads account? ⌄

A standard Google Ads account is where campaigns, ads, and billing live — it is the account that actually spends money. A Manager Account sits above one or more standard accounts and provides a centralised view for reporting, bulk edits, and access management. You cannot create campaigns directly inside an MCC; campaigns must be in a linked standard account.

How do I link an existing Google Ads account to my Manager Account? ⌄

From your Manager Account dashboard, click + Add account and select Link existing account. Enter the 10-digit Customer ID of the account you want to link. The account owner will receive a link request — once they accept it in their Google Ads account settings, the account will appear under your MCC. You can also create a brand-new Google Ads account directly from the MCC without a separate sign-up flow.

Can I access a client's Google Ads account without a Manager Account? ⌄

Yes — a client can grant you access directly via Access and security in their Google Ads account settings without using an MCC. However, this is less scalable: you would need a separate login for each client account. A Manager Account is the preferred approach for agencies because it consolidates access, billing, and reporting across all client accounts in one place.
