How to Add WhatsApp Number to Google Ads Campaigns

Follow these steps alongside the video to set up your WhatsApp Message Asset.

1

Login to your Google Ads account

2

3

Select Message

4

Click the “+” button

5

Add the WhatsApp button to the Account, Campaign or Ad Group level

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about adding WhatsApp to Google Ads.

Can I add WhatsApp to any Google Ads campaign type? ⌄

WhatsApp Message Assets are currently supported on Search campaigns. They are not available for Performance Max, Display, or Video campaigns. You can apply them at the account, campaign, or ad group level.

Does my WhatsApp number need to be a Business account? ⌄

No — you can use a regular WhatsApp number. However, using a WhatsApp Business account is recommended as it lets you set auto-replies, business hours, and a professional profile, which improves the experience for people who message you.

What should I write in the Starter message? ⌄

The Starter message is pre-filled text that appears in the user's WhatsApp compose box when they tap the button. Keep it short and relevant — for example: "Hi, I'd like to get a quote for [your service]" or "Hi, I saw your Google ad and have a question." This lowers friction and helps you qualify leads faster.

Is there an extra cost to use WhatsApp Message Assets? ⌄

Adding Message Assets is free — there's no additional setup cost. You pay only when a user clicks the WhatsApp button, which is billed as a standard click on your campaign. Message Assets don't charge per message received.

What Call-to-action options are available? ⌄

Google Ads provides several CTA options for Message Assets, including Message, Chat, Text, WhatsApp, and others. Choose the one that best matches your brand language and the action you want users to take. "WhatsApp" is the clearest choice if your audience is already familiar with the platform.

Can I add multiple WhatsApp numbers to the same account? ⌄

Yes. You can create multiple Message Assets with different numbers and assign them at different levels — for example, one number for the whole account and a different number for a specific campaign targeting a different region or product line. The most specific level (ad group > campaign > account) takes priority.
