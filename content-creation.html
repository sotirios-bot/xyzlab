Your Questions Answered

Everything you need to know about the AI Content Creation live training.

Is this a live in-person course?⌄

Yes — this is a live, in-person training at our Singapore office. You'll create real content using AI tools during the session, with hands-on guidance from the trainer.

Do I need to be a good writer?⌄

Not at all. This course is designed for people who struggle with content creation — AI removes the blank page problem entirely. If you can describe what you want, AI can help you create it.

What experience level is this course for?⌄

Suitable for all levels — from business owners who have never used AI tools to content marketers looking to dramatically scale their output without sacrificing quality.

What do I need to bring?⌄

Just your laptop and your content goals. A free account with Claude or ChatGPT is helpful to set up beforehand. All other tools and materials are provided during the training session.

Will the course cover video content?⌄

Yes — modules 7–12 cover AI video scripting, voiceover production with ElevenLabs, and editing with Descript. You'll leave with a full video content workflow you can use immediately.
