What's Covered

Every Google Ads Audit Area, Covered

A comprehensive checklist that covers account structure, ad copy, bidding, keywords, and tracking — so you find issues and growth opportunities fast.

✓
Account Structure & Settings

Campaign naming, account organisation, time zone, currency, auto-applied recommendations settings, and linked accounts verified.

✓
Campaign Configuration

Budget allocation, bidding strategy alignment, network settings, geo targeting, ad scheduling, and device modifiers reviewed.

✓
Ad Copy & Creative Quality

RSA ad strength scores, headline variety, keyword pinning usage, and CTA clarity checked across all active ad groups.

✓
Bidding Strategy Review

Smart bidding eligibility, target CPA or ROAS settings, impression share benchmarks, and portfolio bid strategy performance.

✓
Keyword Hygiene & Match Types

Negative keyword lists, search term report review, keyword duplication, low Quality Score keywords, and match type distribution.

✓
Audience & Remarketing Setup

Audience lists active and populated, observation vs. targeting settings correct, customer match lists uploaded and refreshed.

✓
Ad Extensions & Assets

All eligible asset types active — sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, call, lead form, and image assets reviewed for relevance.

✓
Conversion Tracking Verification

All conversions firing correctly, no duplicates, correct attribution windows set, and Google Ads linked to GA4 for audience syncing.
